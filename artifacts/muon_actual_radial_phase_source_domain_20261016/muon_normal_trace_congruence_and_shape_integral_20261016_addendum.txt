Every formula using L_u^-1 or R_u^-1 requires finite, invertible selected
trace amplitudes on the declared regular chart. For the real normalized
eta expression this includes 0<I_eta<infinity, finite positive J_wall,
and nonzero sin(f_wall); existence/normalizability over the actual complete
normal domain is not proved by the local wall data. A zero mode value at
the trace would invalidate this particular inverse-coordinate chart, not
prove nonexistence of the physical field or authorize an arbitrary value.

Border/response inverse formulas are used only at points where the stated
raw bulk border and indicated response inverse exist on the already
declared domain. Their analytic continuation must retain the same domain
and pole structure. No inverse at a singular point is silently assumed.
These are mathematical premises of the exact identities, not new action
coefficients, chosen profiles, additional physical conditions, or a claim
that the remaining global normal-domain realization has been evaluated.
