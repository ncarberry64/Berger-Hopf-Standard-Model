No V-A projection, external mass fit or interacting outgoing law is used.

Tag either neutral daughter at p_tag=(E,E n), |n|=1. The other pair has
q=(M-E,-E n), s=M^2-2ME, electron mass m and a massless neutral. Let
z=m^2/s. In the pair rest frame, integrating its full solid angle gives

  <k_e^alpha p_other^beta>
    = (1-z)(1+2z) q^alpha q^beta/6
        +s(1-z)^2 eta^alpha beta/12,
  <p_other^alpha>=(1-z)q^alpha/2.

For clarity the rest-frame 00 component is s(1-z^2)/4, and the spatial
diagonal mixed product is -s(1-z)^2/12. These two numbers determine the
covariant moment. No numerical pair-angle fit is involved.

Write rho_e[g,c]=H_alpha[g,c] k_e^alpha +m J[g,c], where
  H0=I4, Hi=diag(-sigmai,+sigmai), J=(0 I;I 0),
and rho_other=B_alpha p_other^alpha, B=(I,-sigma).
Then the exact pair spin-density product is

  P[g,c,d,h]=a (H.q)[g,c](B.q)[d,h]
       +b s sum_alpha eta_alpha H_alpha[g,c] B_alpha[d,h]
       +m(1-z) J[g,c](B.q)[d,h]/2,
  a=(1-z)(1+2z)/6, b=(1-z)^2/12.

The final term is the charged-mass OFF-DIAGONAL completeness term. It
must remain for a generic C with left and right charged slots. It is not
recoverable by merely changing the pair velocity in a massless formula.

With U=sqrt(M)(I;I), the nu_mu parent analyzer is
  D[t,s]=C_abcd C^sharp_efgh U[b,s]U^*[f,t]
                        rho_tag[e,a] P[g,c,d,h].
For anti_nu_e it is
  D[t,s]=C_abcd C^sharp_efgh U[b,s]U^*[f,t]
                        P[g,c,e,a] rho_tag[d,h].
Physical C^sharp=C^*; explicit analytic left values/firsts are independent.
Each analyzer includes ALL parent 2x2 off-diagonal entries. The rate is

  dGamma/(dE dOmega_tag/(4pi))
       =E(1-z)/(64pi^3 M) Tr(rho_parent D).

There is no initial spin average in D. A trace-one parent rho supplies
its chosen spin state. The factor M/2 converts this density to the
reduced variable y=2E/M; this is the factor used in the V-A comparisons.
The massive endpoint E=(M^2-m^2)/(2M) has zero pair phase, while the
massless null-pair endpoint is its regular algebraic moment limit.

At fixed kinematics the exact first is the sum of the two bilinear
