2. Complete two-spin instrument and amplitude

Let p_mu=(M,0), k=(E,p n), p=sqrt(E^2-m^2), |n|=1, and q=p_mu-k.
In the retained Weyl basis the parent columns are
  U_mu=sqrt(M) (I;I),
and the electron columns are
  U_e=(t I-sigma.p/t; t I+sigma.p/t)/sqrt(2), t=sqrt(E+m).
Thus U_mu U_mu^dagger is the UNSUMMED parent-spin column map; inserting
rho_mu=I/2 reproduces the existing initial average exactly once. Also
  U_e U_e^dagger=(E I-sigma.p, m I; m I, E I+sigma.p).

For fixed neutral momenta choose either normalized rank-one spinor
factor of rho_n=E_n I-p_n.sigma and rho_a=E_a I-p_a.sigma. Their phases
cancel in every following kernel. The amplitude is
  A[r,s]=sum_abcd C[a,b,c,d] w_n[a]^* U_mu[b,s]
                           U_e[c,r]^* w_a[d].
It retains both electron spin r and parent input spin s.

The unnormalized instrument kernel is
  K[r,l,s,t]=< A[r,s] A[l,t]^* >_neutral_angles.
For any parent rho, rho_e_un[r,l]=sum_st K[r,l,s,t] rho[s,t].
The parent analyzer has D[t,s]=sum_r K[r,r,s,t], so its event weight is
Tr(rho D), INCLUDING complex off-diagonal rho (e.g. sigma2 polarization).
The flattening K[r,l,s,t] -> Choi[(r,s),(l,t)] is positive semidefinite
for a physical adjoint left tensor. This is a quantum instrument, not
a density of a surviving muon after decay. Applying it to one factor of
an entangled parent pair retains the other parent's spin correlations;
no unpolarized covariance replacement is needed.

4. V-A exact analyzer and parity sign

At fixed kinematics the full local functional derivative is

The code implements both. It verifies delta C=iC -> delta K=0 and
delta C=C -> delta K=2K. This alone is not a field/outgoing derivative.

For a supplied real kinematic family the code also differentiates ALL
products U_mu, U_e, N and phase p/(64pi^3M). In an interior momentum chart,
  delta p=(E delta E-m delta m)/p,
  delta t=(delta E+delta m)/(2t),

R/w and its derivative is (delta R-(R/w)delta w)/w. Parent rho firsts
