1. TWO NUMERICAL ISSUES, WITH DIFFERENT CAUSES

The original changing-cell assembly already evaluates the actual incoming
and inherited-child REAL phases at all Gauss times. Its classical EW4/H
test/ghost maps, source clocks, wall strata and matrix identities remain
the same here. Two distinct numerical issues were then diagnosed.

(a) Recovering1-4sigma^2 from binary64 sigma=p-1/2 loses relative precision
near the pole. The original coefficient owner retains the normalized
positive cumulative p and evaluates the IDENTICAL weight as4p(1-p).
This is not a changed action, profile, normalization or fitted parameter.
On every old time snapshot the largest density relative difference was
~9.15e-14 at radial4,4.09e-12 at8, and2.049651164034934e-10 at16.
At32 it reaches1.244565829805523e-8. These local errors must be distinguished
from the much larger radial quadrature errors and the cancellation in
tiny finite-difference signals. The old readouts are not relabeled.

(b) Four temporal Gauss points per tent half integrate only degree7, while
the retained odd ghost bubble (2u-1)u^2(1-u)^2 has a square of degree10.
The constant-coefficient analytic moment is1/6930; the split4-point rule
has relative discrepancy-0.004791135204081237 in that entry. The largest
absolute6x6 moment-matrix discrepancy is8.857709750577890e-6 on the unit
interval. Six points per half reproduce the exact rational moment matrix
to1.110223024625157e-16 absolute in that control; eight points also agree
to roundoff. This addresses polynomial test integration, not convergence
of arbitrary variable coefficients or a physical outgoing realization.

The final production invocation therefore uses EXPLICIT --time-order6,
giving12 times per one-cell history. The source supports numerical time
orders explicitly; its default4 run is retained separately as comparison
evidence. The six-point invocation does not claim a full temporal error
bound for the changing nonpolynomial geometry.
