Consequently a proposed Hodge-Dirac identification has a concrete test.
For K_1 a=(d_A^dagger a,d_A a),
  K_1^dagger K_1=d_A d_A^dagger+d_A^dagger d_A.
The gauge-fixed YM Hessian has the additional F-contact endomorphism.
A plain K_1 square therefore cannot be substituted for that Hessian on
a nonflat background. This is ONLY a check on that proposed substitution:
AE4 does not require P_strat itself to equal the classical YM Hessian.
The heat functional of P_strat generates the local action, whose Hessian
is then a derivative of that functional. The full twisted de Rham operator also has the
off-degree curvature pieces d_A^2=ad(F_A); projecting to one-forms is
another specified operation, not automatic closure. This is an exact
matching identity, not a proof that no microscopic Dirac can match it.

The full384 D5 fermion coefficient map is now constructive, including

body. Its local native factor and positive density proof are separate
from equality to the complete graded D_strat. The full536 real action

scalar and FP constituent forms on one common test/domain complex;
retain gauge-orbit/scalar mixing, all F and off-shell Euler contacts,
regular-interface cotangents, the full fermion complement, and the
already-owned fixed-Y/SM statistics. In parallel bind the explicit

itself, must generate the owned local-action terms in their stated
expansion regime, with boundary/domain contacts and the heat-minus-zeta
replacement retained. No P_strat=classical-Hessian identity is imposed.

This is a concrete assembly/matching problem. The inspected current
sources do not yet evaluate that complete M8 tensor/diffeomorphism-BRST/
twisting/stratified-domain map on the physical background. Older v5/v7/v9
status dictionaries are not used to declare a present theory no-go, and
no nonuniqueness result is asserted here. No new coefficient, reference
state or owner decision is introduced by this review.
