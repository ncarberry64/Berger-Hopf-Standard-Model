Independent review: the AE4 E1 quartic and its physical scalar consumer

The numerical fourth variation of a supplied finite positive form core is
constructible. It is not automatically the intrinsic-Higgs coefficient.

1. Noncommuting fixed-domain fourth variation

Let Gamma(h)=-w Tr E1(c P(h))/2, c>0 held fixed, and write R_j=P^(j)(0).
With r(x)=exp(-c x)/x, differentiating the trace gradient gives

 Gamma''''=(w/2){Tr(r R4)+4 Tr(Dr[R1] R3)+3 Tr(Dr[R2] R2)
                 +6 Tr(D2r[R1,R1] R2)+Tr(D3r[R1,R1,R1] R1)}.

These are Frechet derivatives, not commuting scalar derivatives. For the
quadratic pencil P=P0+h V+h^2 Q/2 in its P0 eigenbasis, this becomes

 (w/2){3 sum_ij r[1](lambda_i,lambda_j) Q_ij Q_ji
       +12 sum_ijk r[2](lambda_i,lambda_j,lambda_k) V_ij V_jk Q_ki
       +6 sum_ijkl r[3](lambda_i,lambda_j,lambda_k,lambda_l)
                                             V_ij V_jk V_kl V_li}.

Repeated spectral nodes use the corresponding Hermite divided differences.
All three terms generally survive. In the commuting scalar limit the
bracket is 3 r' Q^2+6 r'' V^2 Q+r''' V^4, confirming the factors.

with a fourth differentiation of the literal E1 eigenvalue action within

2. Generalized form, length and moving-domain dependence

For a supplied smooth common-domain family K(h), M(h)>0 and c(h), the trace
can be computed from A(h)=c(h) M(h)^(-1) K(h), which is similar to the
positive whitened operator. Obtain its Taylor coefficients through fourth
order by polynomial inverse/product recurrences. An equivalent construction
forms the upper-triangular block Toeplitz matrix with A_j on its j-th
superdiagonal. The (0,4) block of E1 of this matrix is the fourth Taylor
coefficient, without assuming commuting insertions.

The forms must first include their pulled-back trace/domain/quotient and
measure motion. These are total jets, not only bulk K derivatives. The
relative-zeta/eta completion has its own fourth variation. A scalar
variable-M,c control gives fourth derivative -0.00277165808126903672,
while holding both fixed gives 0.01444267865428381106. Thus the omitted
contacts can alter even the sign.
That control uses K(h)=2+.3h+.2h^2,
M(h)=1+.1h+.05h^2+.02h^3, c(h)=.7+.04h+.02h^2, and w=-1.

3. Intrinsic-Higgs compatibility

The saved reduced core probes W_i -> W_i+H with a unit commuting profile.
That is not yet an embedding of the intrinsic weak doublet. The intrinsic
and charged-lepton auxiliary vertices are respectively sigma1_LR tensor
Y_l and sigma1_LR tensor I3 in the existing mixing module. Their mixed
squared contact is 2 I_LR tensor Y_l. The finite four-field kinetic and
mass forms and physical normalized scalar direction remain required.
That requirement concerns the physical renormalized scalar consumer. A
partial fourth variation along the declared intrinsic-Higgs coordinate
can be evaluated before finding the mixed physical scalar eigenline; its
tree kinetic term already fixes its specified bare-coordinate convention.
Only motions belonging to that declared variation enter its total jets.
Fixed-geometry partial H differentiation must not invent geometry motion.

If and only if the first-order carrier, radial profile, clock and domain
embeddings coincide and the unperturbed propagator is family-central,
the formal lepton quartic is kappa_onefamily Tr(h Y_l+s I3)^4. This keeps
all intrinsic/auxiliary mixed terms. Tr(Y_l^4)=1.042607700280037e-8 for
the owned frozen Y_l. The equality is a conditional compatibility formula,
not an evaluation of the intrinsic quartic from the saved reduced probe.

Extracting lambda also requires a declared amplitude convention and Z:
H=h e with ||e||=1 gives Gamma_hhhh/(24 volume Z^2), whereas
H=(0,phi/sqrt(2)) gives Gamma_phiphiphiphi/(6 volume Z^2).
The four-dimensional quartic is logarithmic a4 order in the AE4 owner;
relative completion and common subtraction cannot be omitted as a tail.

A different, constructive intrinsic probe is available on a specifically
held zero-gauge, family-central kinetic core. If D_E is anti-Hermitian,
its L/R kinetic blocks coincide, and B=sigma1_LR tensor Y_l is constant
and Hermitian, then [D_E,B]=0 and
 (D_E+hB)^dagger(D_E+hB)=D_E^dagger D_E+h^2 B^2.
On the common Dirichlet form core the integration-by-parts boundary term
vanishes. In generalized forms this means V=0 and Q=2 M_FE tensor Y_l^2.
The same result follows from the self-adjoint spectral convention
Q0=gamma5 D_E, {Q0,gamma5}=0, with insertion gamma5 M.
The resulting finite fourth variation per explicitly counted core block is
  U4_h=6 w Tr(Y_l^4) sum_i r'(lambda_i).
For h=phi/sqrt(2) this fourth variation is divided by four.
This is not the commuting W->W+H probe. It must remain separate, with its
constant proper-clock profile, held-domain and zero-gauge conditions stated.
Chiral gauge backgrounds D_L!=D_R, spatial/temporal scalar gradients, moving
domain or length, and mass-dependent quotient motion can restore contacts.
Spin/angular/chirality multiplicities must follow the actual saved core,
rather than being inserted once more by hand. This construction evaluates
an intrinsic finite fermion contribution; it does not identify the complete
physical scalar coefficient or replace its classical quartic and matching.
