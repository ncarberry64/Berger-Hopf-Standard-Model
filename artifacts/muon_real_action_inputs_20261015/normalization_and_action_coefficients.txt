dimensionless u2_hat directly in -3 Z u2_hat/u4. The broken branch requires
u2<0 and u4>0. A general nonpolynomial effective potential must not be

3. What cancels from both-spin pole response

At each actual physical simple pole, with physical spin labels s=+/-1/2,
  z_s=l_s Gamma_E r_s,
  E_s,B=-(l_s Gamma_B r_s)/z_s.
Rescaling either eigenvector cancels. More generally replacing the matching
operator by h(E,B) L(E,B) Gamma R(E,B), with h nonzero and L,R invertible,
does not change the pole derivative: derivative terms multiplying Gamma
annihilate the actual left/right nullvectors. Unknown overall operator or
residue normalization must not be manufactured as an additional barrier.

If both spins share a pole at zero measuring field, keep the whole two-spin
kernel rather than prematurely treating it as two distinct scalar roots.
For left/right kernel bases L,R, form Z=L Gamma_E R and M_j=L Gamma_Bj R.
With nonsingular Z, solve (M_j+E'_j Z)c=0. Equivalently normalize the paired
bases so Z=I and diagonalize -Z^(-1)M_j, retaining off-diagonal spin entries.
No positive real collar norm is required for this complex-pole normalization.
The physical spin labels still require the owned rest frame and spin action;
sorting the resulting energies cannot supply those labels by itself.

Under a CONSISTENT uniform unit conversion
  E=kappa E_hat, B=kappa^2 B_hat, m=kappa m_hat,
  E_s,B=kappa^(-1) E_hat_s,Bhat.
For signed charge q and fixed physical spin orientation, define
  Delta_B = d_B(E_- - E_+),
  g = (2m/q) Delta_B,
  a = (m/q) Delta_B-1.
Thus kappa cancels from g and a, whereas each projection moment
mu_s=-E_s,B retains one inverse energy unit. These are readout identities,
not a pole or outgoing-response computation. Complex-pole quantities may
remain complex; no real projection is performed by these identities.

Likewise if a unit-charge connection a is canonically normalized through

then B_can=sqrt(Z_A) B_a. The explicit Z_A factors cancel between Delta_B
and q in this readout ratio. This does NOT remove Z_A from an interacting

4. What does not cancel as a mere unit choice

Rescale the literal retained intrinsic action using
  x_phys=x_hat/kappa, H_phys=kappa H_hat, A_phys=kappa A_hat.
Its dimensionless scalar equation still contains
  D_hat^2 H_hat +2 lambda_H (H_hat^dagger H_hat-nu_hat^2)H_hat
                +Yukawa source_hat=0.
lambda_H is dimensionless and survives. Choosing kappa=nu merely changes
all coordinates/fields together; it leaves the physical product nu R
invariant. Changing nu_action at fixed retained cap geometry is a different
physical theory/candidate, not a change of units.

In the retained canonical quartic model,
  m_H^2=4 lambda_H nu^2, m_mu^2=Y_mu^2 nu^2,
so m_mu^2/m_H^2=Y_mu^2/(4 lambda_H).
The common dimensionful nu cancels from this ratio; the dimensionless
coupling ratio remains. This establishes that a scalar unit cancellation
does not prove independence from the unknown scalar action response. A
specific correlated cancellation among master-action derivatives would
need to be derived from that action; none is supplied by the ledger.

For an m-dimensional worldvolume,
  S_surface=-gamma integral dmu_m,
  gamma_hat=gamma/kappa^m=alpha_FSC (kappa ell_s)^(-m).
The selected mechanics law fixes the exponent and form. A uniform unit
choice does not select alpha_FSC or the dimensionless relative length

alpha_FSC positive symbolic with no selected exact decimal.
