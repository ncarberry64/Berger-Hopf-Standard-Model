## 4. Recovered seam scalar and an exact exterior check

The September galaxy source §3 singles out the mixed boundary scalar

\[
V_\Sigma^2=\frac{c^2}{8\pi}\int_{\Sigma_r}
H_\Sigma\kappa_{\rm acc}\,dA,
\]

where H is the spatial mean curvature and kappa_acc is the normal proper acceleration divided by c². In its weak static spherical limit it gives `VΣ²=GM/r`. The source is explicit that identifying this invariant with an **additional exported scalar-topographic source** is still a BHSM hypothesis. The weak-field metric slip and the coupled topographic threshold are not derived by the mere existence of this invariant.

There is a useful exact extension of the surface calculation that requires no new coupling. It is independently checked here rather than attributed as an already completed historical result. In an isolated nonrotating vacuum exterior, write

\[
ds^2=-f c^2dt^2+f^{-1}dr^2+r^2d\Omega^2,
\quad f=1-2m/r,\quad m=GM/c^2,\quad r>2m.
\]

On a static timelike worldtube of areal radius r,

\[
H=\frac{2\sqrt f}{r},\qquad
\kappa_{\rm acc}=\frac{m}{r^2\sqrt f}.
\]

Therefore the product has an exact cancellation of lapse factors:

\[
\boxed{\quad
\frac{c^2}{8\pi}\int H\kappa_{\rm acc}\,dA
=\frac{c^2m}{r}=\frac{GM}{r},\qquad r>2m.
\quad}
\]

With the common convention `K_ab=h_a^mu h_b^nu nabla_mu n_nu` and outward spacelike normal, `K_uu=−kappa_acc`; a formula using Delta K_uu needs its normal/sign convention fixed rather than silently substituting a positive acceleration. The mixed term in `K²−K_ab K^ab` is then consistently `2H kappa_acc`. The scalar has velocity-squared units, as required.

The static horizon limit of the product is finite, but H tends to zero and the proper acceleration diverges as r approaches 2m. The static worldtube becomes null. This is **not** a dynamical horizon boundary action evaluated at the horizon, and no static-observer construction is extended inside it.

For flat subtraction, the corresponding spherical Brown–York energy is

\[
E_{\rm BY}(r)=\frac{c^4r}{G}(1-\sqrt f),
\]

and the same Schwarzschild mass can be reconstructed exactly:

\[
\boxed{\quad
M=\frac{E_{\rm BY}}{c^2}
-\frac{G E_{\rm BY}^2}{2c^6r}.
\quad}
\]

Thus a tracker using a boundary quantity must distinguish its radius/reference-dependent quasilocal energy from the conserved Schwarzschild mass. Variation of E_BY with choice of enclosing radius is not a new physical mass drift. The two displayed identities were checked with symbolic algebra; both simplification residuals were exactly zero. This is an internal mathematical check, not an observational validation.

The domain is a static spherical local vacuum region with an explicitly flat reference subtraction. An exact global closed-cosmology embedding, rotation, accretion or time-dependent mass requires a consistent metric/stress-energy construction. Those conditions cannot be imported by replacing M with an arbitrary M(t). The boundary representation fixes the ordinary compact mass contribution, while its light propagation is computed from that same metric once.
