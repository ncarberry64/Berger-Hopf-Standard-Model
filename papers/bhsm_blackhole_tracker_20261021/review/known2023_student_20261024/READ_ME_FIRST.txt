BHSM Blackhole Tracker -- compact2023 Student experiment evidence
Formal reviewer source: https://github.com/ncarberry64/Berger-Hopf-Standard-Model

The software ran the original2023 observations and a separately generated
control. Neither completed experiment establishes an accepted known-black-hole
recovery. The publication/scientific-identification hold remains.

Every campaign retains16 original angle/sign starts. Original numerical v2
produced48 endpoints per campaign; seed-centered periodic/trust v3 produced64.
These files preserve all64 start records and224 phase endpoints across the four
campaigns, including every numerically unresolved case. They are not224 objects.

Both native fits have a similar conditional point near7 solar masses, calculated
from a fitted angular scale and parallax. This is not an accepted measurement,
precision estimate or independent recovery of the published black hole. Its
numerical stability between two finite runs does not establish uncertainty or
observational adequacy. All original failures remain and no threshold was relaxed.

The periodic coordinate correction removed the old angular-seam obstructions.
The v3 local refinement then reported a non-positive-definite fifth leading
Hessian minor in12 native starts and one generated-control start. This is a
numerical curvature limitation, not evidence for or against black-hole identity.
Feature-knot counts, exact noise-domain/frame contacts, ranks/KKT scores, all
per-phase statuses/reasons and top projected gradients are inspectable in the
JSON/CSV. The native selected point still contacts the brightness-noise-slope
analysis boundary. A numerical boundary is not a physical confidence limit.

ALL224_ENDPOINTS_v1.csv supplies a compact audit table. ALL64_START_MODES_v1.json
retains every original seed, endpoint conditional parameter state, status and
reason. CAMPAIGN_COMPARISON_v1.json includes aggregate descriptive residuals,
timings, original model-call counts and outcome hashes. Descriptive residual
statistics have no calibrated p-values. No original observation values,
per-row model predictions or observation-level residual arrays are included.
INPUT_BINDING_BEFORE_v1.json and COMPACT_EVIDENCE_RECEIPT_v1.json pin the complete
original external artifacts and source integrity; these paths are provenance
traces, not dependencies needed to inspect the compact results.

The control is one declared generic nonBH engineering state at the actual
observation support, using the same working error law. It is not an independent
calibration sample or a physical classification inferred from a chosen mass.
Its fitted point differs from its generating truth in this one noisy realization;
no bias, interval coverage or classifier success is inferred from that difference.

Still required for an accepted physical interpretation:
1. A prospectively reviewed local derivative/Hessian stability diagnostic and
   a numerically resolved all-mode search. No further fit or derivative diagnostic
   is admitted by this compact export itself.
2. A justified treatment of the noise-domain contact and a correct refitted
   Student adequacy/reference study. Old Gaussian49-bin chi-square calibrations
   cannot be transferred to this chronological Student law.
3. A nuisance-complete mass uncertainty/coverage analysis retaining all modes,
   exact zero/active faces, unresolved regions and domain contacts. A small
   gradient alone supplies neither identification nor a physical mass interval.
4. Clock, exposure-weight, frame, neighbor/seeing and lens/source-light
   sensitivities grounded in actual calibration information. The current positive
   2023 errors are supplied marginal errors; angular independence and equal
   exposure weights are working assumptions. No absent2025 errors are invented.
5. Independent positive/negative/blind validation and separately attributed
   darkness/single-lens evidence before a BH classifier or new-target claim.

Absolute lens distance, absolute velocity/current lens position and microscopic
interface/core/horizon states are not supplied by these fits. BHSM remains
explicit: the ordinary Einstein/GHY optical exterior is one common surface-
topography/envelopment geometry, counted once. Conditional exterior configuration
constraints are useful before BH identity, while the latter remains a separate
evidence question. No additional optical potential or GR anomaly is required to
use that interpretation. This experiment identifies no new black hole.
