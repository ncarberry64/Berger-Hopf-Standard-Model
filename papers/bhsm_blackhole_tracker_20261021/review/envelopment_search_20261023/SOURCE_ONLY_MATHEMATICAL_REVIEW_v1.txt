2. Projected profile diagnostic: mathematical check

Declare stationary scalar geometric optics in the weak-field, single thin
lens Einstein/GR sector; neglect spin/current corrections in this diagnostic.
Weak field does not require weak magnification. Let R be a physical lens-plane
radius, not an observed global surface radius. Let Sigma_reg be an integrable
axisymmetric projected mass density and include any modeled central mass atom
explicitly:

  M_2D(<R) = M_c + 2*pi*integral_0^R Sigma_reg(s)*s ds,
  alpha_hat(R) = 4*G*M_2D(<R)/(c^2*R).

M_c is an effective central point component, or a constant enclosed inner
component only on a ray range exterior to that component. It is not a
declaration that a physical black-hole interior is a material delta density.
The ordinary regular integral without M_c would omit the point-mass case.
The deflection is the physical positive bending magnitude. The reduced
angular deflection additionally contains D_ls/D_s; physical R requires D_l.
No unavailable distance may be manufactured to evaluate this diagnostic.

The primary lens source supplies the projected-density kernel [L, Eqs3.2-3.4,
PDF page47 (zero-based46)] and axisymmetric relation [L, footnote5, printed51].
Own differentiation, for R>0 and positive differentiable M_2D, gives

  d log(alpha_hat)/d log(R)
    = R*M_2D'(R)/M_2D(R) - 1
