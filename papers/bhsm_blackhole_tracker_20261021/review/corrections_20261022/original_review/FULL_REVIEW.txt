BHSM CORE/SURFACE AMENDMENT AND BLACK HOLE TRACKER
Independent review of the completed update — 22 October 2026

REVIEW OUTCOME

The update forms a coherent research release: the formal BHSM repository,
tracker source, installable wheel, manuscript and Museum exhibit agree about
the release and its scientific scope. The existing product suite passes.
The core/surface interpretation is carried into the tracker without inventing
measurements of the core or counting the same gravitational contribution twice.

Four substantive corrections are recommended: three reproducible software
defects and one missing qualification on a theory equation. A fifth, lower
priority item is a source-version reconciliation. These findings warrant a
targeted correction release. They do not require reopening every historical
BHSM calculation or treating all matching constructions as absent.

Priority P2 below means a material correctness or interpretation issue that
should be corrected before relying on the affected output for scientific
inference. P3 denotes a provenance cleanup. No P0/P1 issue was established in
the reviewed scope. This is a review, not an implementation of the fixes.

Finding list:
  F1 / P2: Coordinate conventions alter the fit's physical search domain.
  F2 / P2: Likelihood profiles admit unmarked points outside the physical domain.
  F3 / P2: Unidentified motion is exported with a near-zero public covariance.
  F4 / P2: The radial membrane equation omits its flat/constant-tension assumptions.
  F5 / P3: The source-gap record needs reconciliation with the named handoff.

2. FINDINGS

F1 / P2 — Coordinate conventions alter the physically permitted fit

Location:
  products/blackhole_tracker/bhtracker/inference.py:43–44, 64, 69–75
  products/blackhole_tracker/bhtracker/forward.py:24–29

The center coordinates are restricted to plus/minus 20 computational Einstein
radii around the observed-image centroid. Source coordinates are restricted
to plus/minus 30. The fitted center is nevertheless defined at the user's
chosen reference epoch. Changing that epoch can move the correct reference
center outside the numerical box even though its track at all observed epochs
is unchanged. Changing the numerical reference mass changes the physical sizes
of both boxes.

This contradicts the intended role of a reference epoch and the documented
description of reference_mass_msun as numerical normalization rather than a
physical mass prior. Returning the optimizer's bounds does not make these
coordinate-dependent restrictions into physical evidence against the track.

Reproduction used the existing independently constructed exact 10-solar-mass
point-lens fixture. The measurements, full covariance, physical mass bounds
and explicitly supplied physical worldtube radius were held fixed:

  t_ref     reference mass     fit mass       status          chi-square
  2026      7                  10.000000      TRACKED         5.66e-25
  2000      7                  10.304640      FIT_REJECTED    9628.77
  2026      0.001              11.181477      FIT_REJECTED    26598.72
  2026      100000             10.000000      TRACKED         3.89e-25

In every case the correctly transformed physical truth passes the public
forward model's physical checks and reproduces the measurements to a maximum
absolute difference below 2.31e-14. The rejected cases report an active bound;
they do not silently claim a valid detection. Their rejection still arises
from parameterization rather than a change in data or physical validity.

Recommended correction:
  Fit a center at a stable internal epoch near the observations, then transform
  the center and covariance to the requested reporting epoch. Define physical
  positional bounds independently of numerical normalization, or transform
  explicitly specified physical bounds consistently. Preserve genuine physical
  mass, speed and worldtube constraints.

Acceptance criterion:
  Equivalent reference epochs and numerical normalizations must recover the
  same admitted physical track and objective to the stated numerical tolerance.
  Include a conventional distant reference epoch and small/large normalization
  choices while keeping the physical worldtube fixed.

Evidence: evidence/repro_coordinate_domain.py and its recorded JSON.
Source: [S1], [S2], [S3].

F2 / P2 — Likelihood profiles include physically inadmissible states

Location:
  products/blackhole_tracker/bhtracker/inference.py:238–247

The profile objective calls calculate(..., admit=False). After each nuisance
optimization it records parameter value, chi-square and numerical convergence,
without checking the optimized state's physical admission. All rows enter the
profile normalization. A converged optimizer result can therefore be presented
as an ordinary physical likelihood point even when the public forward model
rejects that same state.

Reproduction starts with the noiseless nearby_motion example, nuisance='none',
starts=1. It sets the declared weak-metric cap to the true minimum-image-impact
GM/(c^2 b) ratio times 1.00001:
  weak_metric_cap = 6.116157051213848e-08.

The baseline is admitted and TRACKED at mass 100000 solar masses. The resulting
mass profile includes these numerically converged points:

  mass                 delta chi-square     independent physical admission
  100000.000000        0                    accepted
  100038.050144        1.000232831          rejected
  100076.114767        4.001862924          rejected
  100114.193873        9.006288306          rejected

For the first rejected point, the actual weak ratio is
6.116174562489037e-08. The public forward calculation rejects it with the
worldtube_weak_metric condition. The profile row has no corresponding flag.

The deliberately tight cap is a domain-boundary regression probe. It makes no
claim about the size of an astrophysical approximation error. The existing
warning about nonregular statistical coverage addresses a different issue:
the physical domain must also be honored or reported explicitly.

Recommended correction:
  Solve the nuisance problem within the admitted physical domain, or mark and
  exclude inadmissible unconstrained optima from physical interval inference.
  Report physical-domain truncation separately from optimizer convergence and
  numerical box truncation. Dropping an inadmissible nuisance optimum alone
  does not compute the corresponding constrained optimum; label that situation
  accurately if a constrained solve has not been performed.

Acceptance criterion:
  An admitted baseline whose profile reaches a physical boundary must not
  return an unqualified physical delta-chi-square crossing outside that boundary.
  Cover the ordinary weak-field and conditional R1 admission paths when fixing it.

Evidence: evidence/profile_admission_repro.py and profile_admission_result.json.
The wrapper only records optimized nuisance parameters; it does not change the
objective or solver and restores the original function afterward.
Source: [S1], [S3], [S4].

F3 / P2 — Unidentified proper motion receives a near-zero exported covariance

Location:
  products/blackhole_tracker/bhtracker/inference.py:133–140, 164, 192

With all observations at the reference epoch, proper motion has no effect on
the predictions. The public forward model produces exactly identical values
for two distinct admitted motions in the reproduction.

The fit correctly reports PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED, null motion standard errors,
null motion signal-to-noise and two unidentified motion directions. However,
the exported proper_motion_covariance is approximately:

  [[9.597701500478059e-35, 5.502449251278180e-35],
   [5.502449251278180e-35, 3.065847069228179e-34]]

These values come from the truncated pseudoinverse on identified directions.
They are not ordinary variances of the unconstrained physical motion. A user
propagating this public matrix can assign essentially perfect precision to a
quantity the observations cannot determine. Scalar errors and the GUI already
handle the lack of identification correctly; the defect is the contradictory
public covariance and its exported representation.

Recommended correction:
  Mark affected covariance entries, or the whole unidentified motion block,
  unavailable. If a generalized inverse on the identifiable subspace is useful,
  expose it separately with the relevant projector/null-space information.

Acceptance criterion:
  Repeated-epoch data must produce consistent unavailable uncertainty in all
  public representations of motion. Preserve finite, valid covariance in an
  ordinary identifiable-motion control.

Evidence: evidence/repro_null_motion_covariance.py and its recorded JSON.
Source: [S1], [S3].

F4 / P2 — The membrane equation needs explicit background restrictions

Location:
  docs/BHSM_NORMAN_CORE_SURFACE_AMENDMENT_20261022.md:184–203
  theory/bhsm_covariant_bubble_interface_mechanics.md:69–86

Equation (6) presents the radial reduction

  gamma [ R_ddot/(1-R_dot^2)^(3/2)
          + p/(R sqrt(1-R_dot^2)) ] = Delta P.

It follows from L = -gamma Omega_p R^p sqrt(1-R_dot^2), assuming a round
spatial p-sphere, fixed Minkowski background, constant tension, coordinate-time
derivatives and c=1. Spherical symmetry on regular geometry is insufficient.

For example, on the regular static exterior

  ds^2 = -f(r) dt^2 + dr^2/f(r) + r^2 dOmega_p^2,  f(R)>0,

the induced Lagrangian is

  L = -gamma Omega_p R^p sqrt(f(R) - R_dot^2/f(R)).

Already at a static surface, the outward-normal mean-curvature contribution is

  K = f'(R)/(2 sqrt(f(R))) + p sqrt(f(R))/R,

which differs from p/R. A changing environmental tension or backreacting
geometry also requires its own terms. The flat-background assumption later
stated for the scalar front calculation qualifies a different derivation.

Recommended correction:
  Introduce Eq. (6) explicitly as the fixed-Minkowski, constant-tension special
  case, with |R_dot|<1 and c=1. For curved or backreacting geometries, direct the
  reader to the covariant traction equation evaluated with the actual induced
  metric. Apply the same qualification to the inherited mechanics document.

This is a scope defect in theory documentation. No current tracker numerical
prediction error has been attributed to this membrane equation.
Source: [S5], [S6].

4. SCIENTIFIC ASSESSMENT

Core and regular spacetime

The amendment preserves Norman P. Carberry's central distinction: the common
core has no primitive spacetime metric, clock, trajectory, volume or propagation
speed. Its proposed mode weights, representation relations and algebraic phases
remain abstract. A mode label becomes a physical frequency or length only when
a correspondence and spacetime-side calibration are supplied.

This still permits mathematical structure in the core description. It avoids
silently putting an ordinary geometric Laplacian or clock into the core, or
relabeling an existing regular child variable as the nongeometric core. The
existing regular incidence, reciprocal attachment and KKT constructions retain
their own scope. The amendment's completion flags remain appropriately open.

Formation, persistence and terminal transition

The inherited spacetime differential, frame dragging, interface formation,
boundary-supported persistence and exceptional terminal core transition remain
distinct claims. The definitions preserve encapsulation as persistent boundary
dominance at a stated scale/environment. The exceptional zero-daughter terminal
route is not equated with ordinary persistent encapsulation.

The restricted nonlinear scalar calculation does demonstrate a possible local
steepening route for compressive data while its kinetic margin stays positive.
It does not yet yield a selected shock continuation, closed stable skin, hard
noninteraction, galaxy formation history or neutron-star endpoint. Its own
source-free stationary-flux result also rules out a nontrivial derivative jump
within the connected positive branch studied. Those are useful scoped results,
and both should remain visible.

The hard-boundary condition is stronger than a transmitting enclosure or a
single input with zero outgoing signal. The terminal zero-daughter proposal
still needs charge/flux accounting and a physical matching law. The quantum,
three-quark and double-slit illustrations remain hypotheses/interpretations;
the current tracker does not test their classification or a universal
interference cutoff. Quiet radiation alone does not identify a horizon-free
neutron-star transition.

What the tracker establishes conditionally

The ordinary fit reconstructs effective optical mass, reference location and
observer proper motion under supplied distances, clock, image association,
covariance, source freedom and lens/environment family. The BHSM Einstein/GHY
interpretation can accompany this fit without requiring a non-GR residual.

The boundary energy and principal extrinsic-curvature deficit are derived
representations of the same mass at a declared radius. Their factors, units,
inverse mass identities and local uncertainty propagation are consistent in
the reviewed static Schwarzschild sector. They do not measure a hard boundary
radius. The mass contribution is counted once. The conditional R1 response
uses its separate declared conventions, and the Schwarzschild boundary identity
is withheld for R1 rather than applied to a different mass definition.

The conditional core-to-optical response formula is also correctly framed:
if a physical correspondence F(Q,a)=0 and an appropriate constrained inverse
K^-1 exist, then D_a Q = -K^-1 D_a F. The optical response follows by the chain
rule, with explicit direct terms retained when relevant. Its distinguishable
part must survive nuisance projection. These equations organize a calculation;
they do not choose the missing physical correspondence. The software correctly
uses unavailable/NOT_INFERRED for core state, transfer rates, daughter channels,
horizon status and the neutron-star endpoint.

Other mathematical checks

No additional discrepancy was found in the checked weighted-median carrier
derivative, regular Schur/KKT response jets, scalar principal symbol and
nonlinearity calculation, connected-positive-branch flux argument, or nonzero
null-wave energy at X=0.

For the tracker manuscript, the checked uniform state bounds, state-inclusive
characteristic-speed bound, rank-three screened STF full-line projection,
two odd projection nulls, harmonic zero-flux rank reduction, first-order delay
null and stated apparent-mass kernel identities were internally consistent
within their assumptions. The bounds concern the retained frozen/quasistatic
principal system. The projection statements concern the stipulated full-line
projection and noncritical primary branches. They do not imply unrestricted
microscopic reconstruction, coupled nonlinear stability or horizon completion.

The galaxy-coherence/dark-matter interpretation remains a proposed physical
connection. The present compact-lens product does not derive galaxy rotation
curves from coherence, select a boundary-formation threshold or measure the
past formation state of a galaxy. Those questions require their own forward
response and observational comparison. They are future scientific work, not
extra software gates needed to repair F1–F3.

interpretation and a research amendment that separates definitions, conditional
derivations and remaining physical hypotheses.
