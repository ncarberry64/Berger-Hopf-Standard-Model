PRIMARY SOURCES AND ATTRIBUTION

1. OGLE-III published standard-event dataset

Wyrzykowski et al. (2015), The Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series,
216, 12. DOI:10.1088/0067-0049/216/1/12.
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.3134
Final v4 text: https://arxiv.org/html/1405.3134v4
Official project/citation page:
https://ogle.astrouw.edu.pl/cont/4_main/len/bulge_o3/
Official archive:
https://ftp.astrouw.edu.pl/ogle/ogle3/blg_tau/
Official optimized photometry directory:
https://ftp.astrouw.edu.pl/ogle/ogle3/blg_tau/PHOT/CLASSA-OPTIMIZED/

The project page requests citation of the catalog paper when using these data.
The archive README refers to its 2014 preprint; use the 2015 journal record above.
Per-file URLs and hashes appear in download_manifest.json.

2. Historical OGLE/MOA/HST benchmark observations

Lam, Casey Y., et al. (2022), author-deposited dataset:
"An isolated mass gap black hole or neutron star detected with astrometric
microlensing", Zenodo, version 1.0.0, 10 June 2022.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6607578
Record: https://zenodo.org/records/6607578

The author's README requests, for HST data, citation of Lam et al. 2022 ApJL
"An isolated mass gap black hole or neutron star detected with astrometric
microlensing" and its corresponding 2022 ApJS supplement. Preserve these
attributions and obtain the complete journal citation from the primary records
when preparing a manuscript.

For OGLE data the README requests:
Udalski, A., Szymanski, M. K., and Szymanski, G. (2015),
"OGLE-IV: Fourth Phase of the Optical Gravitational Lensing Experiment",
Acta Astronomica 65, 1.
https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015AcA....65....1U/abstract

The benchmark bytes are those of the 2022 deposit. The published MD5 checksums
are preserved and verified in download_manifest.json.

3. Later studies: contextual comparisons, not observation inputs in this bundle

Lam, C. Y., and Lu, J. R. (2023),
"A re-analysis of the isolated black hole candidate
OGLE-2011-BLG-0462/MOA-2011-BLG-191".
https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03302
https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/aced4a

Sahu, K. C., et al. (2025),
"OGLE-2011-BLG-0462: An Isolated Stellar-Mass Black Hole Confirmed Using
New HST Astrometry and Updated Photometry".
https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07820
https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/adbe6e

These later works changed reductions/observations. No newer reduced centroid
table or latest full data release is claimed to be included here. Their fitted
masses must not be substituted for observations or imposed on the 2022 benchmark.

4. Later complementary cohort / prior-candidate crossmatch

Official OGLE-III strong-parallax project:
https://ogle.astrouw.edu.pl/cont/4_main/len/oiii-bhs/
Its light-curve dataset is NOT included in this package. The supplied pilot
comes from the standard-event catalog above. Candidate interpretation checks
should consult current literature rather than treating old mass estimates as
confirmed labels.

Mróz and Wyrzykowski (2021), relevant reanalysis:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.13701
