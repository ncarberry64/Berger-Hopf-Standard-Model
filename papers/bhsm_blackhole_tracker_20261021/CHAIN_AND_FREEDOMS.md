## What remains separate

The tool does not uniquely recover the microscopic state or determine an interior, horizon, scalar-flux selection or causal deformation law. Those stronger inferences require their own source/matching relations. They are not conditions for retaining the ordinary fit as a BHSM-described gravitational exterior. Identical exteriors cannot be distinguished by these lens observables alone.

All optical calculations assume geometric optics, noncritical continued primary images, the stated source/flux model and slow local evolution. Unknown source propagation, unrestricted potentials, fast dynamics and uncertain calibration are outside the finite fitted family. Local scalar principal positivity is not full-system or UV stability. Numerical tolerances do not bound every physical truncation, and the formal tiny delay is not certified total-delay accuracy.
