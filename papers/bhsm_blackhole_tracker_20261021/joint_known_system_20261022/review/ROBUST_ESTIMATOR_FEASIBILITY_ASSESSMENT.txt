BOUNDED INDEPENDENT FEASIBILITY ASSESSMENT — NO NEW FIT OR BOOTSTRAP
2026-10-22. Current frozen v2 Gaussian campaign remains stopped/inadequate.

Conclusion

A native weighted nonlinear estimating-equation estimator with cluster/block
robust inference is a principled, genuinely separate research route. Failure of
the frozen Gaussian covariance families does not logically prove that every
correct-mean estimator will fail. It would be too strong to declare new external
observations the only mathematically possible path from the present results.
However, saved G0 does not establish the zero-mean, inter-night independence and
finite/high-leverage angular-cluster conditions needed to accept a reliable mass.
No sandwich mass error, bootstrap sample, mass test or new acceptance result has
been calculated in this assessment.

What the estimator would assume

Let beta contain the nonlinear PSPL/parallax geometry, source fractions and
baselines, theta_E and the relative angular-frame nuisance parameters. Keep the
native reported errors unchanged and use fixed positive working weights W_c.
For cluster c, with physical forward mean mu_c(beta), residual r_c=y_c-mu_c and
Jacobian D_c=dmu_c/dbeta, solve

    U(beta) = sum_c D_c' W_c r_c = 0.

If E[W_c r_c | measurement design] = 0 at the true identifiable mean, covariance
misspecification need not make this estimating equation inconsistent. For
independent clusters with suitable finite moments, enough non-dominant clusters,
and full-rank local identification, a covariance estimator has sandwich form

    A^{-1} (sum_c U_c U_c') A^{-T},

where A is the negative derivative of the estimating equations (or a consistent
correct-mean approximation). This changes uncertainty estimation, not the G0
weighted-least-squares point. It does not make native sigma into a measured full
covariance. Dependence between nights requires a defensible long-run score
covariance/block design, not merely relabeling each night independent.

The method cannot correct a biased mean, calibration error correlated with time,
seeing or a parallax regressor, weight endogeneity, incorrect blend geometry, or
shared reference-frame distortion. It cannot supply absent clock or absolute
frame information, a source distance, or a lens-flux limit.

Saved-data evidence, computed descriptively without refitting

The accompanying SAVED_G0_CLUSTER_ESTIMATOR_FEASIBILITY.json and script record
the exact saved G0 file hash and all definitions. The 12,500 OGLE rows form 1326
local-noon observing nights (size min/median/max 1/5/49). Weighted nightly residual
means have correlation 0.0255 over all successive nights, but 0.1041 among 1093
successive-night pairs separated by less than 1.5 days. This is evidence against
assuming all short-separated nights independent without further assessment;
it is not a calibrated test or proof of a particular long-memory process.

Weighted seasonal residual means include −1.584 mmag (2013), +0.950 mmag (2014),
and +0.846 mmag (2015). Their descriptive night-independence conditional sandwich
mean scales are 0.310, 0.259, 0.230 mmag. These scales omit parameter-estimation and
between-night dependence, so must not be quoted as calibrated significance or
as proof of nonzero deterministic bias. They do show why zero mean conditional
on season/design cannot simply be asserted from a global residual average or
global Q near its nominal degrees of freedom.

There are only 11 calendar visits available as potential HST clusters,
not 42 independent angular coordinates. At saved G0 geometry, the angular linear
design has 8 columns/rank 8. After projecting its other 7 linear nuisance columns,
the partial theta_E information is concentrated: 2011-08-08 contributes 50.74%,
2012-09-09 contributes 19.77%, and 2013-05-13 contributes 14.75%. The inverse
Herfindahl effective number is 3.08 visits. This is an optimistic conditional
design diagnostic holding nonlinear geometry fixed, not a robust effective-DOF
theorem or confidence result. It demonstrates highly uneven leverage. An
ordinary 11-visit pairs bootstrap omits the leading visit in (10/11)^11 ≈ 35% of
draws. A wild scheme retains the design but does not automatically repair
few-cluster leverage, unknown cross-visit frame covariance or asymmetry.

What a genuinely separate prospective assessment would need

1. Declare the estimating target and mean/exogeneity assumptions, physical
   bounds, modes, treatment of native weights and all unidentifiable outputs.
   Preserve this failed Gaussian campaign unchanged; do not redefine its gates.
2. Fix a photometric clustering/block-score rule before its mass outcomes. Assess
   dependence versus time gap, seeing and season. A choice of block length is an
   additional assumption that must be justified and stress-tested, not selected
   because a lower mass bound passes a desired threshold.
3. Treat HST visits jointly within each visit, with small/high-leverage cluster
   correction or an appropriately justified null-restricted wild/score method.
   Common frame calibration may require explicit nuisance terms or calibrated
   cross-visit information;11visits are not an asymptotic guarantee.
4. Validate finite-sample behavior on this fixed irregular cadence, including
   mean/calibration perturbations, heterogeneous/correlated errors, sparse
   angular clusters, boundaries and negative/null systems. Independent reduction
   or calibration checks would strengthen the otherwise untestable mean model.
5. Invert a joint constrained mass test rather than assuming a symmetric delta-
   method error for theta_E/pi_E near a weak or zero parallax. Refit/profile all
   nuisance states and competing modes for bootstrap/null calculations; retain
   infinite/unresolved intervals and failures. No automatic Gaussian Wilks cutoff.
6. Predetermine adequacy, coverage tolerances and a stop rule. If support is not
   reliable under these assumptions, report that outcome; do not iterate until
   the known object's published mass or a BH threshold is reached.

This is a feasibility specification for author evaluation, not authorization or
a freeze of another campaign. Neither the G0 point 6.5457 Msun nor its successful
synthetic/functional replay certifies a robust lower mass bound. Darkness and
single-lens evidence remain separate even if a robust mass test later succeeds.

Primary methodological sources

Liang,K.-Y.; Zeger,S.L.(1986), Longitudinal data analysis using generalized linear
models, Biometrika73,13–22, DOI10.1093/biomet/73.1.13:
https://academic.oup.com/biomet/article-abstract/73/1/13/246001
This establishes estimating-equation inference without specifying the full joint
outcome distribution, under stated mean and dependence regularity conditions.

Cameron,A.C.; Miller,D.L.(2015), A Practitioner's Guide to Cluster-Robust
Inference, Journal of Human Resources50,317–372, author-hosted published PDF:
https://cameron.econ.ucdavis.edu/research/Cameron_Miller_JHR_2015.pdf
Its sectionsII,VI,VII distinguish within-cluster covariance robustness from
between-cluster assumptions, discuss few-cluster reliability, and extend the
argument to nonlinear/GMM models. The nonlinear least-squares case permits
additively separable-error wild schemes; generic nonlinear models need score
methods or other justified constructions. None is a model-free bias correction.

MacKinnon,J.G.; Webb,M.D.(2017), Wild bootstrap inference for wildly different
cluster sizes, Journal of Applied Econometrics32,233–254, corrected author paper:
https://qed.econ.queensu.ca/pub/faculty/mackinnon/working-papers/qed_wp_1314.pdf
This provides relevant primary warnings about cluster heterogeneity/effective
cluster count; its treatment-model results are not asserted as a theorem for
the present nonlinear microlensing estimator.
