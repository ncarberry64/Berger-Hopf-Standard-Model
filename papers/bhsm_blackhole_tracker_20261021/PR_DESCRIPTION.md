# Blackhole Tracker: derive conditional boundary-to-lensing transfer and its blind directions

This new theoretical paper asks which compact-source envelope deformations can be inferred from strong-lensing images, flux ratios and delays. It derives a normalized screened R1 exterior and its common-action metric/optical response, proves projection/statistic blind directions, and evaluates joint-data information after fitting 30 lens/source nuisance coordinates. The leading screened quadrupole can leave the primary-pair delay unchanged and is shear-degenerate in the standard delay–flux apparent-mass statistic.

The manuscript is explicitly conditional: no unique microscopic BHSM-to-R1 map, black-hole horizon flux or physical deformation amplitude is selected. The controlled example is extremely faint; the broad synthetic scan is not a feasible motion campaign. Existing angular and Hopf-charge work is preserved as supporting research, with exact archived provider hashes and bounded recovery reports. PR23 and the HE0435 observational record remain untouched.

Validation: nine-stage offline tracker replay passed; independent radial/projection/estimator and nonlinear primary-image checks; zero-flux degeneracy and short-track controls; independent high-precision nuisance projection; compiled, visually checked PDF. The source package includes reproduction scripts, internal skeptical reviews and a lay report. Historical full-suite evidence is clearly distinguished from fresh focused checks. This is an author-review draft for Galaxies; no journal submission or external contact.
