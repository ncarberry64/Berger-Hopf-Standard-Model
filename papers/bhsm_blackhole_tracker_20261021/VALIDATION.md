## Frozen nonlinear and interval checks

The fixed baseline is `make_example("nearby_motion", noise=False)`: a 10^5-solar-mass compact lens, three sources at three epochs over ten years, and a supplied dense covariance. Its measurement scales and calibration assumptions are experimental inputs, not demonstrated instrument performance. Sources remain free in every fit.

- **C6, nonlinear recovery:** default and two nontrivially perturbed data-based initializers, each with three internal starts. All recover mass/center/motion within the frozen 10^(-5) relative/reference-scale tolerances; maximum noiseless chi2 is about 3.01e-23, below the unchanged 1e-6 criterion. Truth is scored afterward, not passed as an initializer.
- **C7, repeated noise:** all 64 sequential full-C Gaussian draws from PCG64 seed 4932026, one ordinary/shear fit per draw. Exceptions, rejected fits and unavailable intervals remain in the denominator and count as misses. There are no excluded or failed fits. Intervals are local known-C 95% Gaussian intervals, log-Gaussian for mass and additive for center/motion, without reduced-chi2 rescaling.
- **Coverage:** mass 61/64; center x 59/64; center y 61/64; motion x 61/64; motion y 61/64. Exact two-sided binomial tests against 0.95 use the fixed alpha=0.01 for each of five reported coordinates; p=0.2532 for center x and p=1 for the others. Exact coverage intervals are [0.8691,0.9902] for 61/64 and [0.8270,0.9741] for 59/64. No threshold was loosened.
- **Bias limit:** all bias/scatter summaries remain in the receipt. Mean y-motion error is about 3.98e-4 mas/year, roughly 2.75 estimated standard errors of its ensemble mean. Passing the coverage guard does not establish negligible bias or universal calibration.
- **C8, weak-motion control:** the ten-year cosmological fixture returns PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED, motion SNR about 0.00482 and component errors about (0.0121,0.0124) mas/year against true (5e-5,-3e-5). Useful mass information does not imply resolved motion.
- **C11, ordinary mismatch:** omit external shear and obtain FIT_REJECTED, chi2 about 28297.8; admit shear and recover (0.03,-0.018) with chi2 about 1.95e-22. The fixed goodness-of-fit guard is p<0.001. Residuals are not assigned automatically to a novel cause.
- **C9, finite ambiguity:** zero-flux harmonic boundary and ordinary compact quadrupoles have the same potential/gradient/Hessian. Three formal finite comparisons and compensating shifts agree to 2.22e-16; four combined operator columns have rank two. Large coefficients occur only in the nonadmitted potential-operator control. Public prediction rejects the inflated state. The product does not expose a joint free nonlinear fit of both degenerate amplitude pairs, so separate-amplitude interval calibration is not claimed.

These checks establish useful inference within the stated synthetic family. They do not certify arbitrary source trajectories, critical events, uncertain distances/frames, all macro/environment nuisances, global likelihood uniqueness or nonlinear profile coverage.
