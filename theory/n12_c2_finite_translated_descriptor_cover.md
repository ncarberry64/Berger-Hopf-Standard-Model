# N12 C2 finite translated descriptor cover

The repository's recenterable flow-box construction supplies a general
Gronwall/Euler enclosure identity, but its earlier child-state and full-Dirac
objects do not match the C2 signed-descriptor slot.  Only that mathematical
identity is reused here.  Every inserted object is C2-native: the branch-24
selected line, hard-block solve, differentiated soft source, translated
`Delta`, reset-owned proof center, and retained boundary coefficient.

Starting from the second translated ball, each cover row derives its ball
radius from the hard self-consistency root, positive-`c_psi` radius, and
parent action domain.  The signed step is the smaller of a strict dyadic path
fraction and the `log(2)` Jacobi-growth radius.  The new endpoint tube contains
the propagated incoming tube, center-field mismatch, and quadratic Euler
remainder.  Iteration continues until the current majorants no longer close a
next tube; such exhaustion is a proof-majorant boundary, not a BHSM canonical
stop or a physical endpoint.
