# Causal composition with both physical first-derivative attachments

The complete physical endpoint DF envelope already includes its signed
midpoint-coordinate correction and the resulting tensor, storage, and output
cross terms. The physical midpoint incidence certificate adds the remaining
physical DF operand in the left/right endpoint output maps.

The consumer preserves the certified coordinate/tensor and coordinate/storage
terms from the complete endpoint DF envelope. It recomputes the midpoint
output cross term at the same physical coordinates and replaces the endpoint
output terms with the combined physical-incidence/construction bounds applied
to the entire stored, projection-corrected endpoint tensors. No old endpoint
output contribution is retained as an additional summand. All 370 point
certificates and their unchanged output operands are required.

The resulting complete local source is transported through the unchanged
causal maps. Existing assembly/storage bounds remain included, and the frozen
map perturbation acts once on the complete source sum. The resulting polynomial
screen does not recertify Y, Z1, or central/mixed terms. Physical Hessians,
constraint/quotient identification, neighborhood bounds, and contraction remain
open. No missing Hessian is replaced by zero and no measured value is fitted.

The selected endpoint full-output consumer applies the same combined physical
output error to the complete physical endpoint Hessian error ball. It replaces
the old output error bound and retains both incident intervals. In particular,
the physical midpoint DF error multiplies the full endpoint Hessian discrepancy;
their cross term is not omitted. Selected endpoint coverage remains explicit.
