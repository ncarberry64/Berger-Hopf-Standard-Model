# N12 forward compact-interval recentering

Status: `FINITE_REGULAR_INTERVAL_JACOBI_TRANSFER_COVER_AUTOMATIC`.

Let `Y:[0,Tmax)->U` be the unique continuum maximal action flow. Fix a closed
regular interval `[0,T]` strictly before a finite stopping locus, or any finite
`T` on an infinite regular history. Continuity in the strong state topology
implies that `Y([0,T])` is compact. Hence

\[
 B_T=\max_{0\leq\tau\leq T}\|Y(\tau)\|_{S_2}<\infty,
\]

and continuity and positivity of the already-existing domain margins imply

\[
 \delta_T=\min_{0\leq\tau\leq T}\{\hbox{all retained margins}\}>0.
\]

The continuum `K(B,delta)` recentering theorem therefore applies at every
point of the interval. Positive local durations and compactness yield a finite
subcover; uniqueness glues the action flow, its first/mixed state Jacobi
cocycles, the exact `log R4` pullbacks, and the fixed-channel transfer jets.
No a priori global action ball, numerical trajectory cover, terminal return,
or new selector is required for a finite regular pre-stop interval.

This theorem does not make the bounds uniform as `T` tends to infinity. If an
actual reset is hit, the existing reset graph supplies the terminal rule. At
a finite canonical domain stop, regular variational propagation ends and the
existing Friedrichs form closure applies. On an infinite regular history,
every finite exhaustion interval is now covered, but convergence of the first
and second birth-Weyl jets at the Friedrichs limit still requires a global
relative-form bound or an equivalent integrable Riccati/transfer variation
estimate. That infinite-end variational limit is the exact remaining exterior
dependency; Gate 7 remains active and chord 3 remains unauthorized.
