# Universal complete channel ledger

The channel ledger enumerates decay and scattering final-state multisets from
one declared action-owned spectrum.  It enforces exact or modular additive
quantum-number conservation, a declared complete selection rule, interval
threshold kinematics, canonical identical-state symmetry factors, and one
amplitude enclosure per physically relevant channel.

Candidate-inventory completeness and physical resolution are separate.  A
candidate is resolved only when it is interval-closed, has an exact-zero
amplitude certificate, or is interval-open with a strictly positive
amplitude-squared enclosure.  Missing amplitudes, overlapping threshold
intervals, an incomplete spectrum, or incomplete selection rules fail closed.

For a decay initial state, a complete ledger with no open nonzero channel
certifies stability, while any open nonzero channel certifies instability.  A
two-body scattering initial state exposes the complete open-channel list used
by the coupled partial-wave unitarity test.  The ledger stores no experimental
particle assignment and cannot promote current BHSM physics until the frozen
spectrum, action amplitudes, and Gate-7 background exist.
