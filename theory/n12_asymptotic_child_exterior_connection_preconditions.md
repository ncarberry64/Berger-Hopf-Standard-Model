# N=12 asymptotic child-exterior connection preconditions

## Question

Can the certified analytic (R_4\to\infty), (H_4\to H_0>0) branch provide
the infinite Friedrichs child exterior required by the event-centered Gate-7
operator without another blind forward chord?

## What the asymptotic theorem supplies

The complete retained action has one real-analytic local branch for
`epsilon=R4^-2` sufficiently small,

`Z(epsilon)=epsilon X5+epsilon^2 R(epsilon)`.

On that branch no event or canonical stop occurs in a sufficiently small
asymptotic neighborhood and (H_4\to H_0>0).  The weight-seven physical
descriptor has 25 stable and 25 center finite roots and no unstable finite
root.

This is enough to define a candidate infinite Friedrichs exterior *after* a
physical event-generated child is proved to enter that branch or its
controlled basin.  It does not itself prove that connection.

## Why no-unstable linear roots are insufficient

Twenty-five center roots remain.  A zero linear root does not determine the
nonlinear basin: scalar equations with identical zero linearization can have
attracting, repelling, or stationary nonlinear behavior.  The certified
weight-five lift and Briot--Bouquet recurrence construct one invariant branch;
they do not prove an open center-stable basin, a trapping neighborhood, or an
intersection with the AE2 reset image.

The reset tangent has genuine physical radius-Cauchy-jet variation after the
time quotient.  Therefore one stored reset representative cannot be declared
to lie on the asymptotic branch, and the common-scale center cannot be removed
as gauge.  Dimension counting alone cannot prove a global intersection.

## Minimal connection theorem

One of the following action-owned statements is sufficient:

1. a validated forward connection from a nonempty physical reset-quotient
   stratum into a certified trapping neighborhood of the analytic asymptotic
   branch, with the required first geometry-Jacobi family and every retained
   domain margin; or
2. a transverse intersection/degree theorem between the AE2 reset image and
   the center-stable asymptotic manifold, again with quotient geometry jets;
   or
3. a certified finite event or canonical exit furnishing the already-retained
   finite endpoint rule before that neighborhood is reached.

The current repository contains none of these.  The asymptotic branch is not
falsified and the infinite Friedrichs route remains valid in principle.  It
is simply not yet an event-generated child exterior oracle.

No chord 3, selector, endpoint, action term, scale, fit, recurrence assumption,
or frozen prediction is introduced.  Gate 7 remains active.
