The signed `D_Y Delta` calculation at the fixed-`s` reference center is a
local covector ball.  The reference center is exactly stored proof node 1214,
but a proof center is not an exact physical history.  The first required
transport is therefore from that center across the certified incoming
exact-state tube.

On the first matrix-Lohner chart the retained product majorants imply

`||D2 Delta|| <= c2 b + 2 c1 b1 + c b2 + |s| R2`,

with every factor supplied by the same-action moving-cubic, bordered-response,
selected-line, and hard-complement certificates.  The mean-value theorem then
gives

`D Delta(Y_exact) in D Delta(Y_1214) + Ball(0,r_seed+B_D2Delta r_tube)`.

This enclosure is valid, but it is not sign resolving.  Its radius is about
`6.27e-4`, whereas the partial seed norm is about `1.60e-9`.  Equivalently,
the present coarse Hessian bound would require an exact-state radius below
about `1.40e-16`, roughly four hundred thousand times tighter than the
retained tube.

This is not an action singularity, event, or canonical stop.  It is the
specific loss caused by taking norms before the cancellations in the signed
second differential of `Delta`.  The minimal mathematical alternatives are
a direct cancellation-preserving `D2 Delta` theorem or a correspondingly
tighter exact-state localization.  The coarse ball may not be promoted to a
physical force or used to close Gate 7.
