# N12 C2 complete non-scale cb operator

On the fixed positive descriptor fiber, write

\[
\Delta=c\,b+sR.
\]

This certificate assembles `D2(cb)` on the intrinsic non-scale tangent sector.
Each of rows 1 through 97 is evaluated by the outward-rounded one-axis
retained-action interval engine.  Every checkpoint carries one hash of the
engine, row driver, and scientific parents; the merge rejects missing,
invalid, stale, or mixed-fingerprint rows.

The row intervals include the complete signed center terms, both first-gradient
outer products, and the certified first-eigenline-matrix motion.  Their
Euclidean row bounds give a conservative Frobenius upper bound for the
non-scale operator norm.  The available `Db`, `Dc`, and `Dlambda` radii are
checked separately for every row, so the coefficient boxes are bootstrapped
rather than assumed.

Coordinate zero is the physical common-scale direction.  It is not silently
deleted: its pathwise Jacobi contribution is already closed by the exact
common-scale Weyl covariance theorem.  The numerical sweep therefore covers
the remaining intrinsic quotient only.  The suppressed `sD2R` contribution
is certified independently and is added exactly once by the complete
`D2Delta` certificate.
