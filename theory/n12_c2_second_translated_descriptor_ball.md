# N12 C2 second translated descriptor ball

The endpoint tube of the second pole-free segment is enclosed by a new ball
whose root-relative radius is derived from the same hard-block
self-consistency root, the positive `c_psi` radius, and the retained parent
action domain.  Half of the remaining admissible radius is reserved as a
strict continuation margin.

On this ball the parent selected-line gap, Legendre margin, positive lapse,
positive boundary-radius rate, and pole-cancelled `Delta` inequality all
remain strict.  Thus the new tube reaches neither completed encapsulation nor
a canonical stop, and another regular forward descriptor step is available.
