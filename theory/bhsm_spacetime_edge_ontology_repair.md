`FORM_CORE_TRUNCATION_BOUNDARY != CORE_BOUNDARY`,

`FORM_CORE_TRUNCATION_BOUNDARY != CANONICAL_STOP_EVENT`,

`FORM_CORE_TRUNCATION_BOUNDARY != SPACETIME_EDGE`,

`CANONICAL_STOP_EVENT != SPACETIME_EDGE` unless theorem-derived.
