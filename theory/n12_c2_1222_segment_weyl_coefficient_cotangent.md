# C2 1,222-segment inverse-free Weyl coefficient cotangent

At `z=-1` the scalar and two product-Dirac form pencils are evaluated by the
positive backward impedance/Mobius recurrence.  No dense stiffness matrix,
kinetic block, or Euler--Dirac block is inverted.  Reverse differentiation of
the same recurrence gives the cotangent with respect to every node log radius
and every proper-duration element.

The calculation is performed both on the 1,064-segment prefix and on its
1,222-segment exact-fiber extension.  Their difference is a genuine nested
finite-core response increment.  It is not yet the heat-minus-zeta force:
the negative-axis synthesis, reset-quotient Jacobi contraction, and maximal
Cauchy tail remain separate required operations.
