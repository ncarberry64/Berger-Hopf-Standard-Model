# N=12 direct finite-endpoint KKT existence preconditions

## Scope

This note tests only whether the retained AE2 heat/zeta functional already
supplies a direct compactness, coercivity, or degree proof for a regular
finite-endpoint root of the forward-adjoint physical-quotient KKT system.  It
does not test a particular validated boundary-value solve, and it does not
reopen the owner-excluded infinite nonencapsulating formation branch.

## The retained heat regulator is not a coercive exhaustion

For one positive eigenvalue, put (x=\ell_\kappa^2\lambda>0).  The retained
scalar regulator is

\[
 f(\lambda)=-\frac12 E_1(x),\qquad
 f'(\lambda)=\frac{e^{-x}}{2\lambda}>0.
\]

Its endpoint behavior is exact:

\[
 \lim_{\lambda\downarrow0} f(\lambda)=-\infty,
 \qquad
 \lim_{\lambda\to\infty} f(\lambda)=0^-.
\]

Consequently, even the positive, ungraded one-eigenvalue restriction is not a
proper high-energy exhaustion: along (P_n=nP_0), the operator size diverges
while the regulated contribution tends to zero.  A supertrace cannot turn
this scalar regulator into a positive coercive norm.  This conclusion is
narrow: it proves that heat regulation *alone* cannot give the missing global
KKT compactness.  It does not prove that no independent estimate follows from
the complete retained geometry.

The zeta-Casimir term

\[
 \Gamma_{\rm SM,zeta}=-\frac{59}{30}\int_I\frac{d\tau}{R_4}
\]

does not repair this argument on the current theorem ledger.  Turning that
term into a proper exhaustion would require global action-owned radius,
duration, endpoint, and domain-margin bounds that have not been proved.  The
constraint-reduced Legendre energy is identically zero, so it supplies none of
those bounds.

## Distinguish principal coercivity from global KKT coercivity

The existing weighted principal inf-sup certificate is a local linear
estimate on the certified N=12 root ball.  Its own ledger leaves compact
lower-order blocks and the continuum event-child result open.  It therefore
cannot be promoted to compactness of the nonlinear reset-quotient KKT
configuration class, lower semicontinuity of the full replacement action, or
a nonzero degree for the finite-endpoint KKT residual.

## Direct-method requirements still missing

A direct minimax/critical-point proof needs an action-owned nonempty physical
finite-endpoint class together with an a priori compactness or Palais--Smale
type bound, lower-semicontinuity/closedness through the endpoint graph, and a
critical-point theorem appropriate to the indefinite constrained action.  A
degree proof instead needs a bounded open quotient domain, a completely
continuous or Fredholm residual, no boundary zeros, and a computable nonzero
degree.  None of these objects is present in the current repository.

Finite encapsulation ontology restricts which completed histories count as
physical; it does not select a reset-fibre representative, create a terminal
post-reset stratum, or compactify the physical quotient.  The two certified
short chords likewise do not reach an endpoint.

## Adjudication

The heat regulator, the zero constraint energy, and the existing local
principal inf-sup estimate do not close a direct KKT root theorem.  The result
does not show that the retained action has no root and does not justify an
action extension.  The live alternatives remain:

1. certify one regular finite-endpoint forward-adjoint KKT root by a validated
   boundary-value solve; or
2. derive a genuinely new action-owned global compactness/degree estimate for
   that same system.

Gate 7 remains active, Gate 8 remains locked, and no chord, selector, endpoint,
scale, threshold, or frozen prediction is changed.
