# N12 compact-support Weyl variations at a Friedrichs end

Status: `COMPACT_SUPPORT_WEYL_C1_C2_AT_FRIEDRICHS_END_DERIVED`.

Gate 7’s zero-source Euler--Lagrange force is first a weak derivative against
compactly supported retained geometry variations. Let `h,k` have support in a
finite regular subinterval of an infinite maximal history. The proper-time
domain and its Friedrichs endpoint class do not vary, while the already
derived fixed-channel coefficient jets make `P_h` and `P_hk` relatively form
bounded with compact support.

For real `z<0`, the Dirichlet reference pencil is coercive and
`||R_D(z)||<=1/|z|`. If `U_a=gamma(z)a` and `U_b=gamma(z)b`, then

\[
 \langle a,D_hM(z)b\rangle
 =\langle U_a,P_hU_b\rangle+\text{retained contact}_h,
\]

and differentiation once more gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \langle a,D_{hk}M(z)b\rangle
 ={}&\langle U_a,P_{hk}U_b\rangle\\
 &-\langle P_hU_a,R_D(z)P_kU_b\rangle
  -\langle P_kU_a,R_D(z)P_hU_b\rangle\\
 &+\text{retained pair/contact}_{hk}.
\end{aligned}
\]

All terms are finite because the Poisson extensions lie in the Friedrichs
form domain and the variations have bounded compact-support form constants.
Thus an infinite endpoint does not require a uniform transfer-jet limit for
the compact-support `C1/C2` weak Weyl derivatives, and it does not require a
terminal return. Endpoint motion is absent in this variation class.

This proves existence and the exact contraction formulas, not their numerical
or interval values for the actual maximal history. The next step is to
evaluate or enclose the fixed-channel Poisson/Weyl contractions and the common
pair-plus-contact terms on a nonempty negative-`z` region, then assemble the
zero-source weak geometry force. Genuinely noncompact variations needed by a
later global saddle remain a separate obligation. Gate 7 remains active and
chord 3 remains unauthorized.
