# N12 C2 expanded-cover endpoint recenter

The denominator-free sixteen-step `theta` cover ends strictly inside the
certified full-action ball.  Its final predictor is not a physical endpoint or
a canonical stop.  This certificate therefore consumes that predictor and its
entire incoming endpoint tube with the already-derived BHSM pipeline:

1. replay branch 24 and certify a fresh simple-eigenline chart;
2. transfer the retained fixed-descriptor growth and action-derivative bounds;
3. rebuild the bordered hard response without inverting the singular
   Euler--Dirac block; and
4. reconstruct the cancellation-preserving center field and its first jet.

The signed descriptor remains the independently propagated, correlated graph
coordinate.  The binary64 eigenvalue is used only to identify and orient the
selected line.  No response block is zeroed: the bordered response is an
internal action-owned seam component.

On the fresh local ball, the invariant-graph field is

`G(Y)=b(Y) Psi(Y)+lambda(Y) V_hard(Y)`.

Its complete second-variation estimate retains both product blocks,

`D2(b Psi)` and `D2(lambda V_hard)`,

including the graph-only terms `D2lambda V_hard` and
`2 Dlambda DV_hard`.  The correlated positive descriptor interval and a
strictly positive recentered `Delta` interval then advance together through a
matrix-Lohner step; no independent scalar descriptor tube is reintroduced.

The result proves that the old proof-ball edge is removable by an action-owned
recenter and supplies the first forward block on the sheared invariant graph.
It does not prove NHIM capture, reach an event, or close Gate 7.  The next
theorem is iterated sheared recentering to strict capture inclusion or the first
retained canonical stop.
