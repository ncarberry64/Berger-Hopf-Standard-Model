# N12 maximal Friedrichs Weyl exhaustion

Status: `MAXIMAL_FRIEDRICHS_WEYL_VALUE_IS_UNIQUE_CORE_EXHAUSTION_RESET_JET_OPEN`.

Let `q_C` be any nonnegative retained BRST-quotiented source form on the
action-owned maximal post-event child interval.  Its endpoint rule is already
fixed: a later retained event uses its reset graph, while a finite canonical
exit or an infinite/excluded maximal end uses the Friedrichs closure of the
minimal form.  Fix a real resolvent parameter `z=-kappa^2<0`.

On a Friedrichs route, choose increasing compact regular subintervals
`I_T` exhausting the maximal interval and impose zero trace only at the
artificial far form-core truncation boundary.  The spaces of compactly supported form vectors are
nested and their union is a form core for the maximal Friedrichs domain.
The shifted energies

`q_C[u]+kappa^2 ||u||^2`

are uniformly strictly convex.  Monotone/Mosco convergence of the restricted
closed forms therefore gives strong resolvent convergence to the unique
maximal Friedrichs operator.  For a fixed birth trace `a`, the Poisson
extension is equivalently the unique minimizer of this shifted energy.
Consequently its finite-core energies converge to the maximal energy.
Polarization gives convergence of every birth Weyl matrix element.  Because
the retained birth trace space is finite dimensional at fixed channel and
Galerkin level, this entrywise convergence is operator-norm convergence:

`M_C,T(-kappa^2) -> M_C,F(-kappa^2)`.

The construction introduces no terminal condition into the physical theory.
Dirichlet at the moving far form-core truncation boundary is only the canonical compactly supported
core approximation to a Friedrichs closure; no individual finite `T` is a
physical force endpoint or a validation cutoff promoted to one.

If a retained geometry variation is supported in one compact regular
subinterval, the form domain is fixed for every later exhaustion member.
The first form derivative is evaluated on the convergent Poisson minimizers,
and the mixed second derivative uses the uniformly bounded shifted
resolvent.  The already-derived compact-support formulas therefore converge
with the values.  Thus the maximal negative-`z` Weyl value and its compactly
supported weak `C1/C2` jets are action-owned even when the maximal end is
infinite and `R4` has no global upper bound.

For the constant comparison operator `-d_tau^2+mu^2` with birth value one,
the finite-core flux is

`M_T=-mu coth(mu T)`,

and the Friedrichs half-line value is `M_infinity=-mu`, with exact error

`|M_T-M_infinity|=2 mu/(exp(2 mu T)-1)`.

This theorem closes existence and uniqueness of the exterior value as an
action-owned exhaustion.  It does not numerically enclose the actual N12
limit, prove convergence of a noncompact reset-parameter Jacobi variation,
control changes of maximal-outcome stratum, synthesize the E1 multiplier at
zero energy, or sum the internal angular channels.  The earliest remaining
Gate-7 input is therefore the first physical reset-quotient jet of the
maximal coefficient/form family (or an equivalent forward-adjoint weak

No selector, endpoint, contour, vacuum, scale, fit, recurrence, gate, or
