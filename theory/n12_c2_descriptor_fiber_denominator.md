# C2 descriptor-fiber denominator theorem

The regularized outgoing C2 equation is solved as a graph over the signed
selected eigenvalue,

`s = lambda_event(Y)`, `Y = Y(s)`.

Consequently an endpoint enclosure at fixed `s` is not an arbitrary action
ball.  It is the intersection of that ball with the exact level set

`lambda_event(Y) = s`.

Its tangent errors satisfy `D lambda_event(Y) delta Y = 0`.  Over one
descriptor step `[s0,s1]`, the exact soft factor in

`Delta = c_psi b_psi + lambda_event R`

is therefore bounded by `s1`.  Replacing it by
`s1 + Lip(lambda_event) r` admits a normal direction that is absent from the
physical descriptor fiber.

At segment 1128 this isotropic replacement is the sole cause of the displayed
`Delta` collapse.  Restoring the exact fiber leaves a strict denominator;
the remaining scalar limitation is hard-bundle wrapping.  The next proof
object is therefore a conjugated matrix/Lohner enclosure of the fixed-`s`
tangent hard bundle.

This restriction is an identity of the existing reparametrized equation.  It
does not add a selector, constraint, time direction, endpoint, or physical
parameter.
