# Gate 7 retained component-separator audit

Let the exact formal reflection be

\[
  \mathcal R(q,v,\ell,\beta)=(q,-v,\ell,-\beta).
\]

The retained action is invariant under this map.  On the reduced
Euler--Dirac variables its symmetric Hessian therefore obeys the exact
congruence

\[
  D(\mathcal RY)=P D(Y)P,
  \qquad P^2=I=P^TP.
\]

Consequently its determinant and Sylvester inertia are reflection-even.  The
Dirac zero set is an existing stopping locus, but its determinant sign cannot
distinguish the certified child from its reflected forward-time partner.
The same row-parity calculation sends the constraint/reset Jacobian to
(SJP).  Its canonical normal Gram determinant is even.  The fixed-event
child block is rectangular (31\times98), so selecting a signed maximal
minor would add a basis-dependent orientation not owned by the reset.

The simple selected real eigenline supplies an action-owned projector, not an
oriented vector: \(\psi\) and \(-\psi\) are the same line.  A reference-overlap
sign is only a chart convention and can cross zero while the eigenline remains
simple.  No closed retained history is available to define a winding class.

Canonical momentum and the eta shift current are reflection-odd, but they are
covectors.  The reset matches momentum without choosing its sign, and the
shift constraint flips with the shift current.  No action-selected scalar
orientation converts either covector into a sign barrier; their natural norms
and quadratic contractions are even.  The boundary Casimir and the fixed
spatial degree/orientation/FR/incidence data are already reflection-even.
Finally, the reset is an equivariant set-valued relation with a 67-dimensional
fixed-event child fiber, not an oriented single-valued child map.

The differential and Noether representations do not rescue a separator.
The odd product (K=c_\psi b_\psi) is a certified nonzero label on the local
singular event component, but no retained theorem keeps it nonzero throughout
the regular child component.  Away from the event,

\[
  \frac{d}{dt}e_{\rm ord}^2
  =2c_\psi b_\psi+2e_{\rm ord}R(Y)
\]

contains the uncontrolled remainder.  The constraint energy is identically
zero, the odd shift current is not a conserved scalar, and the complete
boundary-flux cycle ledger is undefined.

Thus no separator exists in the five prescribed retained candidate families
or their current differential/Noether representations.  This canonically
closes only the separator-first route; it is not a no-go for terminal-event
reachability and not an action incompatibility.

The first specific nonlinear owner in the surviving global-control route is

\[
  D(Y)^{-1}b(Y)
\]

in the Euler--Dirac vector field.  Existing Moser and inverse estimates bound
it only on (K(B,\delta)).  The retained energy does not bound (B), and no
current action identity protects the positive inverse margin \(\delta\).
Gate 7 therefore needs a global bound for this product and the associated
strong/domain margins, or a separator-free integrated event-transport
inequality forcing one of the already canonical stopping alternatives.
