# Point-Hessian sensitivity of the tested trial action

Longitudinal point samples do not test every transverse tube direction. Reuse
the complete independently paired physical point Hessian at the identical
endpoint anchor, weights, reference, normalized branch, and original graph.
Verify the entire upper-triangle inventory and its independent receipt.

Contract D2F at that point with the fixed trial column E_j and each of the 75
scaled tube directions: the longitudinal interval axis and all 74 transverse
Euclidean axes. For each output coordinate, report the absolute longitudinal
coefficient and Euclidean norm of transverse coefficients, with outward Arb
arithmetic. These are supports of the POINT LINEARIZATION of DF E_j, not uniform
derivative variation bounds over the tube.

Compare the predicted longitudinal changes with the independently paired
samples at +/-3/4 radius, retaining uncertainty in both operands. The residual
is a discrepancy at those sampled points only. No neighborhood remainder is
bounded, no larger variation elsewhere is excluded, and no global contraction
is established. This diagnostic consumes existing paired Hessians and samples;
it evaluates no new action derivatives and uses no measured data or fitting.
