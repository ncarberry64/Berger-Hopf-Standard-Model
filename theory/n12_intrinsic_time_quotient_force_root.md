`T=ker D C(y)`, and let `G=span{g_tau}` be the exact coupled whole-system
time-translation orbit.  The physical tangent is `T/G`.  The replacement

`Gamma_rep=Gamma_heat-Gamma_SM^zeta`

`q_rep(g_tau)=0`.

`[q_rep]=0 on T/G  <=>  q_rep(v)=0 for every v in ker D C`.

`q_rep in range(D C^dagger)`,

or to the raw bordered KKT multiplier equation

`q_rep+D C^dagger delta_lambda=0`.
