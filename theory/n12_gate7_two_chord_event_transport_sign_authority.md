# Gate 7 two-chord event-transport sign authority

The two certified chords rigorously enclose the retained forward state on 128
subspans and preserve every existing physical/domain margin.  Their Green
certificates do not, however, certify the sign of

\[
 F_u(Y)=\frac{d}{dt}e_{\rm ord}(Y)^2
 =2c_\psi b_\psi+2e_{\rm ord}R_{\rm ext}(Y).
\]

The certificate rows contain absolute state-shadowing source bounds and local
radius comparisons.  They do not contain signed interval enclosures for
\(c_\psi\), \(b_\psi\), \(R_{\rm ext}\), or \(F_u\).  Consequently, neither
monotone decrease toward the terminal chart nor monotone increase away from
it follows from the current two-chord certificates.  Successful state
shadowing is not transport-sign authority.

The next calculation is bounded and does not require chord 3.  On each of the
existing 128 subspans, reuse the Hermite centers, action balls, hard Kato gaps,
selected-line transport, Green radii, and retained D3--D5 majorants to form a
cancellation-preserving interval

\[
 [F_{u,i}^-,F_{u,i}^+].
\]

The signed center terms must be added before the variation radius is applied.
If every upper endpoint is negative and the accumulated decrease reaches the
certified terminal chart, event entry or an existing stop follows.  If any
lower endpoint is positive, an immediately global decreasing-rate theorem is
invalid on that subspan.  Intervals containing zero require a piecewise
integrated estimate rather than another local chord.

This is a missing proof enclosure, not a retained-action incompatibility.
