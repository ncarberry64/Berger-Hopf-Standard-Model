# N12 C2 fresh descriptor-fiber eigenline chart

The 1,192-segment continuation reaches the boundary of its original action
chart with all physical margins strict.  This is a proof-chart limit.  The same
retained action is therefore recentered at the final predictor tube.

The ambient action ball has one role only: uniformly certify the selected
branch, complement gap, lapse, and positive radius rate around the uncertain
endpoint.  It does not authorize arbitrary ambient motion.  Physical
propagation remains on the codimension-one fiber `lambda_event(Y)=s`, whose
tangent is `ker D lambda_event`; normal motion is fixed by the implicit fiber
graph.

At the fresh center, the action `D3` columns give the scalar, selected-to-hard,
and preconditioned hard-block first variations.  Retained mixed `D4` bounds
control their ball variation.  The chart radius is derived by increasing the
trial radius until the first eigenline/domain theorem failure, then solving for
the maximal strict feasible radius with those same outer-ball bounds.  No
validation cutoff or physical stopping rule is introduced.
