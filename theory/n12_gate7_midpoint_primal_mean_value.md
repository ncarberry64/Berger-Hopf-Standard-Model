# Primal values on the actual Hermite-Simpson midpoint domain

The paired midpoint domain is the exact anchor plus five parameter groups:
two longitudinal intervals, two 74-dimensional Euclidean balls, and a
99-dimensional box containing the endpoint-field and rounding remainders.
Its 249 directions and all five radii are taken from the paired domain
certificate. Each column is scaled by its group radius exactly once;
support then uses unit-radius groups. Every radial segment stays in this
convex product domain and in its certified state hull.

On that hull, the retained smooth action has positive inertia. The paired
62-row strict inclusion and contraction inequalities give a unique oriented
index-24 eigenpair and invertible implicit Jacobian. The physical norm has
a strictly positive lower bound. The same implicit-function argument used
for endpoint tubes therefore applies on this actual midpoint domain; its
certificates are checked under their own midpoint scope.

The producer computes the three complete point solves of the retained rate
graph. Uniform line and response variations use the complete signed
preconditioned derivative equations, the paired inverse bounds and every
scaled direction. For each primal coordinate y, integrate these uniform
first variations on the radial segment from the exact midpoint anchor:

    y(domain) subset y(anchor) + [-support(Dy), support(Dy)].

The group support is the sum of the two interval bounds, the two Euclidean
dual norms and the box absolute sum. The full verified point enclosures,
rather than only their midpoints, anchor the result. The projected line
border is auxiliary; it is not an eigenvalue derivative. Lambda is retained.
Only action derivatives through order three are needed for this step.

A separate producer uses an independently reproduced pair of these primal
bounds in the original complete 99-column midpoint first-variation graph.
It verifies matching source bindings and retains whichever certified primal
coordinate enclosure is narrower. This does not claim physical Hessians,
full-path contraction, a physical quotient, Gate 7 closure or BHSM completion.
No empirical parameter is introduced or fitted.
