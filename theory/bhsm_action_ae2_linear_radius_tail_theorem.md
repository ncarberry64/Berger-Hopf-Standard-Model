# AE2 exact linear-radius tail theorem

An infinite regular history with an exact linear tail `R4=v(tau+tau0)` has factorized superpotential `s_chi=chi*beta/x`, where `beta=|mu|/v` and `x` is a translated dimensionless tail coordinate. The squared factors are the Bessel operators

`K_+=-d_x^2+beta(beta+1)/x^2`, `K_-=-d_x^2+beta(beta-1)/x^2`,

with their natural action graph `(d_x+chi beta/x)u(1)=0`. Exact Bessel combinations give the compact first-vertex threshold laws.

For positive chirality set `nu=beta+1/2`, `m=beta-1/2`, and `D_m(k)=(J_m(k)^2+Y_m(k)^2)^(1/2)`. The delta-normalized state is

`psi_k(x)=sqrt(kx)[Y_m(k)J_nu(kx)-J_m(k)Y_nu(kx)]/D_m(k)`.

The recurrence `(d_x+beta/x)[sqrt(x)Z_nu(kx)]=k sqrt(x)Z_m(kx)` proves the natural graph at `x=1` and gives the factor image without numerical differentiation. Standard small-argument Bessel expansions produce the powers below. Negative chirality is the supersymmetric partner with `nu=|beta-1/2|` and the corresponding adjacent Bessel order.

For positive chirality, the cumulative source measure is `O(Lambda^(3/2-beta))` below `beta=1/2`, `O(Lambda^(beta+1/2))` above it, and `O(Lambda/|log Lambda|^2)` at the critical value. Negative chirality gives `O(Lambda^(beta+3/2))`. Hence the exact E1 Dini integral `integral lambda^-1 d|nu_h|` is finite for every `beta>=0`. The critical logarithm is integrable and must not be mislabeled an infrared divergence.

Here `k=sqrt(lambda)` is only a mathematical Bessel threshold variable. No physical momentum interpretation of neutral `z` is introduced.

Together with the prior integrable-radius theorem, this closes superlinear and exactly linear infinite tails. The actual N12 radius asymptotic class remains unknown; sublinear or nonasymptotic positive-radius tails remain the live direct-theorem class if the action does not force a closed class.
