# C2 finite-core negative-axis Weyl family

The 1,064-segment C2 proof-center path supports more than the earlier `z=-1`
probe.  For every real `z<0`, each scalar element has

`k=sqrt(c exp(-2x)-z)>0`

and each product-Dirac element has

`k=sqrt(W^2-z)>abs(W)`.

Consequently all hyperbolic element transfers are real.  In the scalar
Möbius recurrence every numerator and denominator is positive.  In the
product-Dirac recurrence, `abs(W)tanh(kh)/k<1`, so both diagonal transfer
factors are positive; positive terminal impedance is therefore mapped to
positive birth impedance.  Starting from the analytically eliminated far
Dirichlet form-core truncation boundary proves by finite induction that the response has no
pole anywhere on the real negative axis.

The recurrence is a finite composition of analytic maps.  Hence the
finite-core `M_C2,T(z)` and its reverse coefficient cotangent are real analytic
for every `z<0`.  This gives complete negative-real spectral-parameter
coverage for this proof-center form core without a matrix inverse or stiffness
Schur subtraction.

The order-one uniform log-radius cotangents of the two chiral channels cancel
at low probes, but arbitrary precision retains a nonzero paired remainder of
order `10^-31`.  Binary64 is therefore valid for neither declaring exact
cancellation nor integrating the residual.  The stored multi-scale rows are
crosschecks of the parametric theorem, not a spectral quadrature.

This result supplies the C2 negative-axis diagram slot only on the finite
proof-center core.  It does not supply the sharp parent `M_f`, the actual reset
quotient Jacobi pullback, a sector-complete pair-plus-contact trace, or the
maximal C2 tail.  Thus it is not yet the joint AE2 seam or the heat-minus-zeta
force, and the form-core truncation boundary is not reclassified as a
physical endpoint.
