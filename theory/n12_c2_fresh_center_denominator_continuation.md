# C2 fresh-center exact-denominator continuation

The 1064-segment C2 prefix stops because two sufficient proof reserves become
simultaneously sharp: the hard Jacobi self-consistency was capped at `1/2`,
and the selected cubic sign radius retained one half of its birth value.
Neither number is in the BHSM action or in the regularized descriptor
equations.

At a fresh proof center the inverse-free hard estimate requires only

`1 - gap^{-1}(D3_center + D4 r)r > 0`.

The regularized flow additionally requires positive `c_psi`, `b_psi`,
`Delta`, lapse, and outward radius rate.  The intersection of these open
inequalities is monotone in the local action radius.  Its upper endpoint is
therefore derived by bisection.  The proof ball is the midpoint between the
incoming endpoint tube and that feasibility endpoint.  This midpoint has no
role in the physical equations.

The center values of `b_psi` and `c_psi` are reevaluated from the retained
action.  The cubic uses repeated complex-step contractions and the retained
fifth-variation majorant; all physical-state uncertainty remains in the
incoming tube and is propagated by the local Lipschitz bounds.  The signed
selected eigenvalue is carried from the certified descriptor coordinate,
not recomputed from an ill-conditioned binary64 eigenvalue.

The resulting segment is still a finite regular prefix.  Exhaustion of any
proof radius is not an event or a canonical stop.
