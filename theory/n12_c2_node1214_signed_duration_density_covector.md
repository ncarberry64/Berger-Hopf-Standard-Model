# N12 C2 node-1214 signed duration-density covector

\[
q_\tau=\frac{N_{\partial}s}{\Delta},\qquad
Dq_\tau=q_\tau\left(D\log N_{\partial}-\frac{D\Delta}{\Delta}\right).
\]

coordinates.  The complete non-scale `DDelta` theorem supplies a signed
centered action-dual ball on the node-1214 tube.  Integrating its norm over
that same convex tube gives a sharp positive interval for the value of

are substantially sharper than the older scalar field box because they retain
the cancellation already proved in `DDelta`.

\[

\]

ball, produces a centered signed ball for `Dq_tau`.  Both its centered-ball
margin and an independent reverse-triangle lower bound must be positive.
