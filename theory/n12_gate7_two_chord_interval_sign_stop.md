# Gate 7 two-chord interval-sign three-representation stop

All 130 retained Hermite centers on the two certified chords have positive
diagnostic squared-event rate.  The minimum is
\(4.723663927953948\times10^{-14}\).  This does not yet prove positivity on
the exact tubes.

Three proof-equivalent interval representations have now reached the same
owner.  A full-coordinate interval Euler--Dirac solve fails on the first
subspan with Neumann factor \(5.7819648\times10^6\); it loses dependency
before the signed event contraction.  The pole-free covariant hard-bundle
form preserves that contraction but lacks the between-center D5/Kato
variation.  The implicit-adjoint systems close at their retained centers but
require the same between-center modulus.

The canonical missing object is therefore

\[
 M_{D5,Kato,i}=\sup_{Y\text{ in tube }i}
 \left|D\left(2c_\psi b_\psi+2e_{\rm ord}R_{\rm ext}\right)[Y]\right|
\]

in a mixed representation that preserves signed cancellation.  It must be
small enough, after multiplication by the subspan/tube radius, to exclude
zero relative to the signed center value.  No current representation proves
that inequality.

This is a finite stopping result for the present invariant calculation.  It
does not prove the exact rates positive, prohibit a later return, establish an
infinite history, or show an incompatibility of the retained action.  A new
action-owned cancellation identity or genuinely sharper mixed D5/Kato
modulus is required.  Box refinement, precision escalation, chord 3, and new
physics are not authorized substitutes.
