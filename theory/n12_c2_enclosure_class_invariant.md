# N12 C2 enclosure-class invariant

## Physical class versus proof boxes

The corrected BHSM history-class theorem identifies two regular histories
only when they lie in the same connected component of the action-defined
admissible history space and share the retained discrete, topological, and
boundary labels, modulo the existing gauge and time-origin actions.  A local
validation-box index is absent from this equivalence relation.

For the actual reset-selected C2 history define the discrete enclosure
signature

`Sigma_enc(C2) = (AE2 action/domain version, forward history component,
forward orientation, reset-glued Spin x G_SM bundle class, fixed boundary
incidence/topology class, transported selected-eigenline class, retained
constraint/Noether level-set class, admissible-domain component)`.

Continuous state coordinates, proof centers, proof-box indices, tube radii,
and continuously varying Weyl coefficients are not entries of this discrete
signature.  The scalar and two product-Dirac channel labels are a fixed
operator decomposition attached to the class; their numerical response is
continuous within it.

Every row of the certified 98-segment C2 cover retains transported branch 24,
positive signed and proper duration, positive pole-cancelled `Delta`, hard
regularity, and the inherited lapse, boundary-radius-rate, Legendre,
selected-line, constraint, and reset-domain margins.  The AE2 trace graph and
action version do not change.  The corrected history theorem then keeps the
topological, boundary-incidence, and conserved/discrete labels fixed.
Therefore all certified boxes are representatives of one enclosure class:

`C2_ENCLOSURE_CLASS_INVARIANT_ON_CERTIFIED_CONTINUATION = TRUE`.

## Class-change surfaces

A proof-box boundary or computational safety count is not a physical
transition.  A class change requires an action-owned event/reset stratum,
loss of selected-line simplicity, Euler--Dirac/Legendre/constraint
regularity, a physical lapse or domain boundary, a topology/boundary-incidence
singularity, or a separately certified loss of enclosure support.  The last
support/stability condition is still open as an action theorem.

## Finiteness boundary

The repository contains finite conditional ingredients: four `(C,sigma)`
sector projectors, three charged-family slots, finite frozen `(k,j)` ledgers,
one physical forward orientation, and finite channel/representation ledgers.
It does not yet prove that these ingredients are independent factors of the
physical quotient, attach every factor to the C2 history, bound every
admissible topology/mode-support class, or distinguish all classes by an
action-derived response.  Thus the certified C2 prefix has exactly one class,
but global finiteness and the complete BHSM transition graph remain open.

Hindsight: the 98-box Zeno frontier is proof resolution, not physical BHSM
structure.  The physical result is class invariance while all transition
surfaces remain uniformly open.
