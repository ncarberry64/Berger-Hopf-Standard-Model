# Physical field bounds from paired uniform derivatives

Let F be the already certified physical field on an endpoint tube, z0 its
fixed anchor, V the full 75-column scaled affine direction matrix, and D
an enclosure of DF on the entire tube. The tube is a product of one
longitudinal interval and a 74-dimensional Euclidean ball. For any allowed
parameter u, its radial segment remains in the tube and

    F(z0+V u)-F(z0) = integral_0^1 DF(z0+t V u) V u dt.

Multiply the signed interval derivative matrix by V before taking the
group support. Both groups have unit radii because V already includes
the frozen longitudinal and transverse radii. Add this symmetric support
to the full verified point field enclosure, including its uncertainty.
The paired eigenpair and physical norm certificates establish the same
smooth normalized branch used by the complete uniform derivative.

The resulting bound and the previous normalized field bound enclose the
same field. Selecting the narrower bound separately in each output
coordinate is valid. It does not tune any physical parameter.

Independently reproduce the field bounds before using them to construct a
new actual Hermite-Simpson midpoint outer domain. A smaller new domain does
not automatically inherit an eigenpair proof with a different anchor;
subsequent certificates must bind the new domain and be re-established.
This step does not claim full-path contraction, a physical quotient or
Gate 7 closure.
