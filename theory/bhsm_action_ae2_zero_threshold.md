# BHSM-AE-2.0.0 zero-threshold no-shortcut theorem

AE2 fixes the matter transmission domain, but it does not supply a positive
zero-energy boundary mass. In each retained product-Dirac channel,

\[
 A_s=\partial_\tau+s(\tau),\qquad q_0[u]=\|A_su\|^2.
\]

For every birth trace `g` and every positive-duration collar,

\[
 u(\tau)=\exp\!\left(-\int_0^\tau s(r)\,dr\right)g
\]

satisfies `A_s u = 0`. Its factorized form energy, birth conormal, and
terminal inward graph all vanish. The terminal graph is therefore within the
previously admitted nonnegative class. The same construction can be made on
both sides of the AE2 seam with `g_child = U_R g_event`; both local conormals
remain zero after pullback by the unitary reset lift.

Consequently, positive proper duration and positivity of the spatial Dirac
eigenvalue do not imply a strict zero-energy two-sided Wronskian margin. An
argument that replaces `||A_s u||^2` by
`||u'||^2 + s^2 ||u||^2` drops the action-owned cross term and is invalid.

At a negative probe `z=-kappa^2`, the form is instead

\[
 \|A_su\|^2+\kappa^2\|u\|^2,
\]

so the existing coercive two-chord bounds remain valid. They cannot be
relabeled as a zero-energy certificate.

This theorem does not assert that the realized maximal AE2 exterior has a
zero mode. It proves only that the certified local collars cannot decide the
zero threshold. Gate 7 still needs the action-owned maximal event/child
Calderon maps and their geometry jets. Frozen predictions remain unchanged;
Gate 7 is active, Gate 8 is locked, chord 3 is unauthorized, and
`FULL_BHSM_COMPLETE = FALSE`.
