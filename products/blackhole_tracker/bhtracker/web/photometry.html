BHSM INTERPRETATION
One configuration, one optical potential
The ordinary compact-lens response has an explicit BHSM Einstein/GHY interpretation. The gravitational contribution is counted once.
The photometric crossing time obeys tE = θE / μrel. Duration alone does not measure lens mass. Mass, lens distance, absolute proper motion, a horizon and the nongeometric core remain unavailable here.
A successful light-curve fit does not validate BHSM core, envelope, encapsulation or dark-matter mechanisms. A failed simple model is not evidence for a black hole. Annual parallax and joint astrometry require separately verified clock, ephemeris and reference-frame information.
