BHSM INTERPRETATION

One exterior optical response, counted once.

In BHSM, surface topography includes gravitational spacetime geometry; it is not limited to a material skin or a separate membrane.

When observations and the admitted model support a gravitational origin, the inferred exterior is interpreted as BHSM spacetime-side surface topography. One Einstein/GHY exterior optical response is counted once; no additional force or mass is introduced and no non-GR residual is required.

This is a model-conditional interpretation. A fitted or nonrejected light curve alone does not validate a gravitational origin, uniquely verify BHSM or identify a black hole.

A unique microscopic interface shape, distinct dynamical surface response, hard-isolation state, horizon and core process require additional physical maps and evidence.

What makes a useful next observation?

Parallax and an angular lensing scale can constrain mass under an adequate model. Lens-light limits and checks of stellar, remnant, binary and variability alternatives help identify the object’s type. A precise mass is not required to keep a qualified follow-up target in view.

No entry in this atlas establishes a new black-hole interpretation. Historical published interpretations retain their source and assumptions.
