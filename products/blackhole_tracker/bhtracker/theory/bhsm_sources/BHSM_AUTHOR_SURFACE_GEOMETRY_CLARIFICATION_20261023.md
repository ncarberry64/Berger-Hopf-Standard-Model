## Common frame, distinct configurations

The [8 September clarification](BHSM_AUTHOR_AETHER_SCALE_CLARIFICATION_20260908.md)
describes everything as Aether: stretched Aether is spacetime/support surface,
and condensed Aether is energy. Its field and harmonic language connects
spacetime support, organized matter and gravitational geometry. The author's
common frame extends across excitation, envelope, encapsulation, decay,
collision, daughters and de-encapsulation at different scales.

These names retain distinct roles. A propagating excitation is not
automatically an encapsulated particle; envelopment, boundary dominance,
persistence and terminal transition are different questions. Universality of
the architecture does not require one trajectory, shell shape, threshold,
environment or physical mechanism for every sector. The
[current realization scope](BHSM_CURRENT_ENCAPSULATION_SCOPE.md) preserves
that distinction. The September labels “3D”, “4D”, “core distance” and
“higher harmonic” still require their declared mathematical realization.

The [22 October amendment](BHSM_NORMAN_CORE_SURFACE_AMENDMENT_20261022.md)
continues to control core and endpoint meanings. The common core has no
ordinary metric, distance, duration, volume, density or clock. Surface
topography does not introduce a spatial core interior. The existing
[local enclosure/state bridge](../theory/ae4_current_c2_physical_enclosure_state_integration.md)
does supply a regular, same-spacetime `sigma=0` carrier and state transport in
its stated domain. It is neither a numerical cutoff nor, by itself, hard
isolation, a horizon, a spacetime edge or terminal transfer into the core.
It would be incorrect to restore a blanket claim that no carrier or
matching construction exists.

## What the tracker can usefully establish

The tracker first asks what the observations constrain about a configuration.
When the evidence and admitted model support a gravitational origin, its
inferred gravitational exterior is interpreted as BHSM surface topography.
This can locate or characterize a configuration before its unique physical
type is established. Useful intermediate results include source/event
directions, conditional event-shape constraints, and, where the observations
identify them, angular structure, mass and motion. Each has its own evidence
and identifiability requirements.

A conditional photometric fit is not by itself validation of a gravitational
origin. Nonrejection of a scalar noise/model screen does not exclude source
variability, an ordinary stellar lens or other alternatives. Timescale and
blending constraints do not generally determine lens mass, distance or
absolute motion. Geometry/configuration inference and black-hole identity
are separate inferences; unresolved quantities remain unavailable. Existing
“Potential black hole” investigation labels are work categories, not new
validated gravitational imprints or discoveries.

The adopted Einstein/GHY compact exterior remains one configuration. Its
optical mass, curvature and boundary-energy representations count the same
response once. A supplied enclosing radius is not a measured object skin or
horizon. The optional R1 response retains its own action, matching and
admission domain; this clarification supplies no additional optical force,
field, fitted coefficient or R1 amplitude. A supported exterior does not
uniquely reconstruct microscopic interface dynamics, core harmonics, hard
isolation, daughters, a horizon or a terminal rate.

Accordingly, replace blanket wording such as “no BHSM surface deformation
measured” with the specific result: which gravitational imprint or conditional
configuration is constrained, whether a gravitational origin is supported,
and which finer interface/core properties or object identities remain
unidentified. Inadequate and unresolved fits retain their original status.
