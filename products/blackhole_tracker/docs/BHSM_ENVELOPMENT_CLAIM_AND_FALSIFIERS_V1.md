## Forward model and required data

The existing chain is configuration and matching data -> full Weyl potential
`W=Phi+Psi` -> projected lens potential `psi` -> observed images, fluxes,
delays, unresolved photometry or calibrated astrometric centroids. A source
using half-Weyl `W_topo=(Phi_topo+Psi_topo)/2` contributes `2 W_topo` to the
same full potential. This convention conversion creates no second response.

For the ordinary single thin lens, the dimensionless relations are

`beta=theta-grad psi`, `A=I-Hess psi`, `mu=1/det A`,
`phi(theta,beta)=|theta-beta|^2/2-psi(theta)`.

Physical delays also require the declared time-distance factor. The scalar
sector assumes weak metric, geometric optics, a sufficiently stationary
single lens, continued noncritical image branches and consistent source,
clock, angular-frame and distance conventions. Differential extinction,
source variability, lens/source multiplicity, luminous blending, finite
sources, parallax, environmental shear/convergence, calibration and noise
must be included or justified as negligible in the chosen comparison.
No missing covariance, resolved image, flux ratio or interimage delay is
manufactured from an unresolved light curve.

The source/event catalog direction is not a lens position. A fitted angular
track is conditional on lens association, supplied covariance, reference
frame and parallax treatment; motion alone is neither mass nor BH identity.

### An operational compact/extended diagnostic

Assume a stationary, nonrelativistic, axisymmetric weak-field thin lens in
the Einstein sector, neglect mass-current terms, and let `R>0` be physical
impact radius. For central compact mass `Mc>=0` and regular projected density
`Sigma(R)>=0`, excluding that compact component,

`M2D(<R)=Mc+2 pi integral_0^R Sigma(s) s ds`,
`alpha_hat(R)=4G M2D(<R)/(c^2 R)` [P1].

The compact term can be treated as constant only outside its support. At a
differentiable radius with `M2D>0`,

`d ln alpha_hat/d ln R = 2 pi R^2 Sigma(R)/M2D(<R)-1`.

For two radii `0<R1<R2`, define the annular mass proxy

`C12=R2 alpha_hat2-R1 alpha_hat1
     =(4G/c^2) 2 pi integral_R1^R2 Sigma(s) s ds >=0`.

For an entirely compact lens sampled outside its support, `C12=0`.
The dimensionless alternative is
`Q12=ln[(R2 alpha_hat2)/(R1 alpha_hat1)]`; it is nonnegative for this
positive-density family, and zero outside all projected support. Nonzero
positive density somewhere in the annulus, with positive integral, gives a
strictly positive contrast. A density value at a single point need not do so.

For `z=(R1,R2,alpha_hat1,alpha_hat2)`, the contrast gradient is
`g=(-alpha_hat1,alpha_hat2,-R1,R2)`. If the **complete** joint covariance
`Cz` is supplied by a valid reconstruction, first-order variance propagation
is `g^T Cz g`. Retain all correlations and nonlinear uncertainty when needed.
No uncertainty or significance is assigned when the reconstruction/covariance
is unavailable. Merely evaluating C12 on a best-fit monopole returns zero by
construction; that is not an independent test of the monopole.

The reduced angular deflection includes `Dls/Ds`; an overall common geometry
factor cancels from Q12 and the contrast sign, when it is genuinely common
and positive. Recovering deflection from image data still needs source/center
and nuisance information. Two image radii do not automatically give two
independent deflection measurements. Source positions inferred under the
tested monopole cannot be fixed as if independently known.

These expressions specify a useful **conditional response test**, not a
new extended-halo inference route in the installed app. A future test must
fit the competing forward predictions to native observables, reoptimize
their original physical nuisance domains and retain failed/unresolved modes.

### Restricted spin-two test and lost information

The existing projection derivation gives, in a screened overlap and a fixed
potential convention, `p2(R)=q+u/R^2+v R^2`. With `D=R d/dR`,
`(D^2-4)p2=-4q`. At three distinct positive radii, `xi=Ri^2`,

`det rows(1,1/xi,xi)
 =-(x2-x1)(x3-x1)(x3-x2)/(x1 x2 x3) !=0`.

Two radii cannot determine all three coefficients. At `R=1,2`, the nonzero
coefficient vector `(q,u,v)=(-5,4,1)` gives zero values at both radii;
at `R=3` it gives `40/9`. This proves nonuniqueness from two samples without
a numerical optimizer. The actual matched transfer `T(R)` must replace the
constant approximation and have its rank/conditioning checked in the real
observation design. Three image radii are not three measured potential values.

For one frozen full-line view of a radial STF quadrupole, the `xz` and `yz`
integrands are odd along the ray and vanish. Two 3D state components are
unidentified even before fitting lens nuisance. A second independently
calibrated viewing geometry or a proved dynamical/source relation would be
needed to access them; finite endpoints do not remove the nulls by assertion.

The paper also proves `K_1(u)=K_(R^2)(u)` for its first-order apparent-mass
statistic: constant spin two and shear are indistinguishable by that statistic
alone. Full image geometry, changing source angle and independent center/
distance constraints can add information in specified designs, but must pass
the full nuisance-projected rank test. This is not a finite-perturbation
equivalence of all lens observables. An arbitrary distributed quadrupole can
also mimic the restricted radial response; q is not a unique BHSM identifier.

### Dynamics/lensing closure that needs upstream theory

This example requires a stationary spherical three-dimensional scalar
exterior with negligible mass-current contribution and weak metric on the
retained domain. In this paragraph Phi/Psi have velocity-squared units;

`Psi=gamma_slip Phi`, with constant slip over the relevant ray region.
If the ordinary Poisson relation applies, this total dynamical potential
corresponds locally to `rho_total=Vflat^2/(4 pi G r^2)`; rotation alone
does not establish that spherical three-dimensional density or identify
its material content. Baryonic and any already represented effective
components must be counted once.

In the ideal full-line logarithmic calculation,
`integral_-infinity^infinity R/(R^2+z^2) dz=pi` gives bending in radians

`alpha_enc=(1+gamma_slip) pi Vflat^2/c^2`.

Thus a prospective fixed-realization residual is
`F_slip=c^2 alpha_enc/(pi Vflat^2)-(1+gamma_pred)`.
An unbounded logarithmic potential is not a globally weak finite-mass
galaxy. Inner/outer truncation, the actual line-of-sight path and matched
exterior give corrections that must be derived before applying this
ideal relation to observations; finite endpoint corrections cannot be
discarded merely because the rotation curve looks flat. In the Einstein
sector with negligible anisotropic stress the potentials coincide;
any distinct slip needs its own admitted field/constitutive realization.
No extra fitted slip is added here.

The residual can be used only after action, physical metric Hessian, source,

### Existing boundary-differential example

For `m=GM/c^2`, a static sphere at a **declared** areal radius `r>2m` has
one-principal-curvature flat-reference deficit
`delta k_g=(1-sqrt(1-2m/r))/r` and
`m=r^2 delta k_g-r^3 delta k_g^2/2` [P2]. This is half the extrinsic trace
deficit, not intrinsic Gaussian curvature `1/r^2`, spin, an Israel junction
jump or a measured material boundary. The same M is represented once.
An ordinary spherical body has the same vacuum exterior outside its support;
a positive reference deficit therefore does not imply a black hole.
Changing r changes the representation, not the conserved exterior mass.
No static observer is continued through a horizon by this dictionary.

## How to turn a response claim into an acceptance test

Before a measured test, freeze the observational version/selection, clock,
frame, source association, original observations/errors/covariance, competing
response families, action-owned coefficients, all physical nuisance domains
and branches, quality checks and decision criteria. Compare full native
predictions; validate noise/model adequacy and numerical/reference stability.
Use a prospectively justified sampling distribution, negative controls and
held-out predictions or suitable refitted simulations for the actual design.
Do not use a fitted-model-derived contrast as an independent datum, choose
thresholds after seeing desired outcomes, or convert numerical search failure
to zero likelihood. Report unresolved cases and trials/multiplicity.

The companion external controls use ideal invented values to verify the
contrast/gradient, finite-support counterexample, boundary inverse,
mass-sheet identities, STF parity, restricted radial rank, conditional slip
identity and duration scaling. They supply no classification threshold,
calibrated coverage, native likelihood admission or new scientific result.
