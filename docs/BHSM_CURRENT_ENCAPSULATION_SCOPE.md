# BHSM current encapsulation and realization scope

BHSM currently has one owner action, `BHSM-AE-2.0.0`. Universality belongs to the action and its admissibility mechanism. It does not imply that every physical manifestation shares one numerical trajectory, environment, scale, boundary condition, or interaction history.

The retained 371-node Gate-7 center is one action-selected background realization and proof carrier. Its exact center, canonical earliest stop, correlated Green scalar, causal 512-bit central response, and decisive mixed/transverse seeds remain authoritative. Gate 7 is still active: the mixed polarization map must be extended to all endpoints and midpoints, the full transverse unit sphere must be majorized, and the longitudinal/transverse two-radius test must then be completed. This scope clarification changes none of that mathematics.

The repository already transports preserved particle, family, representation, projector, and current data into a local carrier/state-enclosure interface. That is a derived local bridge. It is not yet a theorem that the complete interacting AE4 child contains a nonzero spacetime volume, nor does it identify the Gate-7 trajectory with every electron, quark, neutrino, hadron, atom, or other state.

Current classifications are deliberately separate:

- A topologically closed fermion-like state with zero spacetime interior is a research hypothesis requiring a same-action closure and zero-interior theorem.
- A complete interacting spacetime-volume enclosure remains open.
- Hadronic organization as the first spacetime-volume regime is a hypothesis, despite the available color-singlet response interface.
- Atomic and macroscopic enclosure is a hypothesis until a composite bound-state theorem is supplied.
- An electron pair may be an ingredient of a coherent many-body state, but the pair alone is not a superconducting state.

Encapsulation stability is also not yet a lifetime. A physical decay prediction requires one environment-conditioned realization, its constrained quadratic Hessian, dressed poles and residues, renormalized vertices, LSZ states, a complete channel ledger, outward phase-space integration, and optical-theorem reconciliation. The generic machinery exists, but the action-owned physical instance is open.

Measured masses, couplings, mixings, lifetimes, and calibration residuals remain comparison-only. The required order is derive, certify, freeze and hash, predict, then compare.
