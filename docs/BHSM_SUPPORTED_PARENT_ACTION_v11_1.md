# BHSM v11.1 Supported Parent Action

Every v11.0 term is retained with its domain, coefficient, field dependence,
support dependence, and boundary/core incidence. The formal family has the
shape `S_sup[w,lambda_D]=sum_a integral chi_{w_a}(upsilon)L_a dmu_a` plus the
canonical depth kinetic term and declared boundary pieces.

Because the primitive support representations and measure characters are not
owned by the action, this is a family rather than one complete physical action.
No coefficient, field, particle datum, or weight is fabricated. The Euler,
stress, current, and junction expressions therefore remain formal functions of
the unselected representation data.
