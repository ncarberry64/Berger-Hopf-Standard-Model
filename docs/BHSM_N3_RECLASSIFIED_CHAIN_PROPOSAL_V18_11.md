exact projected v17.61 map.  It is not revalidated or promoted here.  Because
its earlier trial produced substantial independently verified descent, the
same model may be used only to propose new states.

Every proposal is evaluated in the square 376-variable KKT system with its
event multiplier explicit.  The multiplier is not analytically eliminated.

path taken by the previous iterate.  Promotion requires only reduction of the
independently evaluated total physical merit, the eta domain, and a complete
persistent child.  Thus no solver rule becomes a physical equation.
