# BHSM N=3 action-owned stiffness measurement v18.14

This audit freezes the exact accepted v18.12 state and measures, rather than
assumes, the proposed microscopic-stiffness mechanism.  It changes no KKT
equation, event definition, coefficient, eta domain, or complete-child gate.

The unchanged exact local action jet is evaluated along a reconstructed-radius
family.  Its spatial-gravity, intrinsic-curvature, cosmological, eta,
ADM-kinetic, Hopf-inertia and boundary-Casimir contributions are separated,
and their radius powers are fitted rather than imposed.  Exact local Hessian
subblocks measure scale, u/w/v, velocity, lapse and shift curvature.

The local curvature spectrum is nondimensionalized only with the existing H6
product weights, the period-induced velocity scale and the action's own term
norm.  Eight representative global directions are then probed with the exact
376-component square-KKT residual at action-owned displacements of 1e-6 and
1e-4.  Raw step size, action change, residual response, eta admissibility and
radius dependence are recorded separately.

The artifact classifies whether an order-1 action displacement inferred from
the dimensionless spectrum is itself comparable to 1e-6.  It does not promote
physical stiffness merely because a raw or unnormalized condition number is
large.
