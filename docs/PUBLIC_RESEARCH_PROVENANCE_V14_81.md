# Public research provenance — v14.81

The scientific report was cross-checked against public literature on:

- Ashtekar & Krishnan dynamical-horizon energy/area flux balance laws.
- Sberna et al. nonlinear black-hole ringdown and absorption-induced mode
  excitation.
- Faraoni & Rinaldi on black-hole horizons embedded in time-dependent
  cosmological geometries.
- Farrah et al. / Croker et al. cosmologically coupled black-hole dark-energy
  proposals.
- Rodriguez; Andrae & El-Badry; Ghodla et al. constraints and astrophysical
  consequences of strong cosmological black-hole coupling.
- Green & Wald and the subsequent backreaction debate.

The package does not treat contested cosmological-coupling claims as established
facts.  They are used only to motivate a testable BHSM response calculation.
