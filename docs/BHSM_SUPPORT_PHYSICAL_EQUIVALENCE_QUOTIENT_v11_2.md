# BHSM v11.2 Physical Equivalence Quotient

For provisional representatives A and B, the regular map
`Phi_B=upsilon^(Delta w)Phi_A` is invertible and its derivative terms are now
organized by the composite connection. It is singular at the core. Fixed
distinct real characters are not linearly naturally isomorphic.

This does not prove physical inequivalence. Weyl, measure-redistribution,
canonical, spectral, domain, and observable comparisons require complete
actions and domains on both sides. Physical equivalence and inequivalence are
both withheld.
