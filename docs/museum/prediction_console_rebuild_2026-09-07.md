# Prediction-engine Museum consoles

The public Museum presents BHSM's historic ambition, then five animated science
consoles, one data/research exhibit, academic details, Author and other work.
The academic repository remains the source of reproducible scientific authority.

## Scientific meaning of the animations

- **Matter from geometry:** linked Hopf fibers are stereographically projected
  from S3 into R3 and displayed in a rotating view. The three-family particle
  map is schematic. The retained conditional SM bundle and all 34 ledger entries
  remain accessible within the exhibit. This animation does not solve a vacuum
  or derive a particle mass.
- **Collision theatre:** incoming beams, an interaction and outgoing tracks form
  an eight-second sequence. Three two-body demonstrations use the tested
  four-momentum calculator. Recorded CMS dimuons use the retained 64-event sample.
  The incoming beam drawing is schematic; the outgoing transverse paths are
  scaled illustrations, not detector hits. No BHSM amplitudes or rates are invented.
- **Magnetic fingerprints:** all four retained CODATA 2022 particle moments are
  shown simultaneously with their signed values and uncertainties. There is no
  cursor or bar magnet. A common 0.1 T field and f = 2|mu|B/h provide physical
  precession readouts. Moment arrows have normalized lengths, and individual
  animation frequencies use logarithmic compression for visibility.
- **Prediction test:** the ten frozen sandbox screens meet independently sourced
  published comparisons. The reveal animation does not represent a new numerical
  solve. The theta23 tension, Higgs difference, edition qualifications and
  available reference uncertainties remain accessible. No comparison feeds
  upstream into BHSM normalization or mode choice.
- **Forces unifying:** flowing ribbons follow the author's specified branching
  topology into an aether core. Reference energy scales retain their different
  definitions; they are not an increasing energy axis. Infinity remains an
  explicitly conceptual endpoint, not a calculated physical divergence.

The original CMS animation is the default in the separate data/research exhibit.
The Author and other-work markup and content are retained. The cosmic animation
has only a lint annotation and an unused callback argument removed; its visual
behavior and scientific labels are unchanged.

## Motion, data and progression

Scene clocks pause outside the viewport and in hidden tabs. Global pause,
individual controls, keyboard selectors and reduced-motion preferences are
supported. Data labels remain visible; technical records are in expandable
science drawers. The approved-output intake and all source-owned promotion gates
are unchanged. An empty intake no longer generates repeated placeholder cards.
New physical outputs still require independently reviewed derivations and the
existing fail-closed source checks. Full BHSM physical closure remains open.
