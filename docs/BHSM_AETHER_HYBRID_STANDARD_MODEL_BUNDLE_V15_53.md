# BHSM v15.53 — Standard Model bundle on the hybrid child

On every open hybrid segment the faithful gauge group is

\[
 G_{\rm SM}=\frac{SU(3)\times Sp(1)\times U(1)_Y}{\mathbb Z_6}.
\]

The \(\mathbb Z_6\) generator is

\[
 (e^{2\pi i/3}I_3,-I_2,e^{i\pi/3}),
\]

which acts trivially on every selected fermion multiplet and on
\(H=(1,2)_{1/2}\).

One rank-16 family is

\[
 Q_L=(3,2)_{1/6},\quad L_L=(1,2)_{-1/2},\quad
 u_c=(\bar3,1)_{-2/3},\quad d_c=(\bar3,1)_{1/3},
\]

\[
 e_c=(1,1)_1,\qquad \nu_c=(1,1)_0.
\]

The three copies are the three (C_3) triality eigenbundles. Hypercharge is
not selected by anomaly cancellation alone when \(\nu_c\) is present, because
an anomaly-free (B-L) admixture remains. BHSM removes that continuum with
the geometric operator

\[
 Y_{BH}=-\frac12I+\frac23P_C+\frac12S_\sigma
 -\frac12P_wS_\sigma,
\]

normalized by (Y_{BH}(H)=1/2\). It yields the multiplets above, all four
Yukawa hypercharge sums vanish, all local gauge and mixed gravitational
anomalies vanish, and the global (Sp(1)) doublet count is even.

At actualization, connection one-forms are not carried as pregeometric
primitives. The representation labels, triality projectors, hypercharge
operator, bundle isomorphism class, and FR parity are discrete gluing data;
the post-event zero-background connections are reconstructed in the same
bundle class. The mass/Yukawa matrices and renormalized couplings remain
operator evaluations, not free entries silently inserted here.
