\[

\[

\]

mathematical language at this contact layer.

\[
(\deg\eta,\epsilon_{\rm orientation},(-1)^{\rm FR},

\]

density, and canonical metric momentum are not transported as primitive

\[

\[

\]

\[
\Sigma_c^-\longrightarrow\Sigma_c^+,\qquad
\Sigma_p^-\longrightarrow\Sigma_p^+.
\]

\((\Sigma_c^-,\Sigma_p^-,\Sigma_c^+,\Sigma_p^+)\), is

\[
\partial_1=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1&0\\
0&-1\\

\]

No child/parent exchange occurs. Only the contact layer crosses the
firewall; the regular child interior and its \(S^3\times S^3\) full-preimage
boundary remain the reconstruction seed. Metric evolution resumes only on a
post-cut branch with positive eta Legendre form.
