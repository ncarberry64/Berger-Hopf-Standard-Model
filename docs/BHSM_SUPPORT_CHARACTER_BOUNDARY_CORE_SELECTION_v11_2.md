# BHSM v11.2 Boundary/Core Character Selection

Finite-boundary GHY, scalar, gauge, fermion, support-normal, compatibility,
and normalized-measure routes were tested. They provide known Green/flux
forms but no action-owned ensemble or character equation.

At the core, `upsilon^w=exp[-w q_D/lambda_D]` decays, remains constant, or
grows according to the sign of `w`. Finiteness cannot select a sign without
the core measure, response functional, asymptotic profiles, and self-adjoint
domain. Finite action, energy, symplectic flux, and support flux therefore
remain unevaluable rather than assumed.

The forced-zero matter candidate is trivially compatible with existing gauge
anomaly cancellation. A nonzero chiral support Jacobian is undefined without
a nonzero charge ledger and regulator-defined symmetry. Boundary, core, and
anomaly tests do not select the five null directions.
