# BHSM v15.89: cycle scale and renormalization map

The single dimensionful datum is

\[
 \ell_\kappa=\kappa_1^{-1/6}.
\]

The logarithmic mean radius of the controlled cycle defines its matching
scale without a new parameter:

\[
 \log\mu_{\rm cyc}^{-1}
 =\frac1{T_*}\int_0^{T_*}\log R_4(t)\,dt,
\]

\[
 R_{\rm cyc}=1.02082779\,\ell_\kappa,
 \qquad
 \mu_{\rm cyc}=0.97959715\,\ell_\kappa^{-1}.
\]

At that scale the same \(\Gamma_{\rm cyc}\) gives the transverse and electric
components of one nonlocal DtN form factor. Extending the action-owned weak
normalization with the single rank-16 carrier trace gives

\[
 K_Y:K_2:K_3=\frac53:1:1
\]

in both components, with

\[
 K_2^T=K_3^T=3166.083808,
 \qquad K_2^E=K_3^E=2345.290877.
\]

These are not two independently tunable Maxwell coefficients. They are two
components of the same boundary operator. Nor are they automatically the
zero-momentum coefficients of a local four-dimensional Yang--Mills action;
that identification requires the low-momentum expansion of the DtN operator.

One-loop transport from the matching scale is

\[
 K_i(\mu)=K_i(\mu_{\rm cyc})
 -\frac{b_i}{8\pi^2}\log\frac{\mu}{\mu_{\rm cyc}},
\]

with \((b_Y,b_2,b_3)=(41/6,-19/6,-7)\). The trace ray is a matching condition,
not an RG-invariant ray. RG transport introduces no new matching coefficient
and cannot shift the gauge and Yukawa outputs independently: a scheme change
must act on the common \(\Gamma_{\rm cyc}\).

BHSM therefore fixes the absolute dimensionless residues in
\(\ell_\kappa\)-units. It does not derive a number of meters or GeV from
dimensionless mathematics; such a number is the numerical value assigned to
the single dimensionful action datum \(\kappa_1\), not a missing gauge or
Yukawa normalization.
