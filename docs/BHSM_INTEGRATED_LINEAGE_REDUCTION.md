# BHSM integrated lineage reduction

All fetched BHSM branch lineages have been substantively integrated into `main`.
The integration rule is not that older work is disposable. Every lineage is a
BHSM asset; unique files remain in the current tree, while conflicts on a shared
path retain the newest adjudicated semantics.

## Canonical reduction

1. Existing particle/family/mode, representation, projector, current, and
   topological results are reusable upstream assets at their original claim
   strength. The reconstruction does not rebuild the particle spectrum.
2. The AE2 selected stop is the `lambda24 = 0` reduced Euler–Dirac Hessian stop.
   Its event child is a derived geometric/dynamical object.
3. A BHSM family or mode may manifest as an SM particle through the existing
   manifestation/readout architecture.
4. The missing identification bridge must carry that frozen state through the
   stop and event child into an action-owned local enclosure and its existing SM
   manifestation class.
5. The remaining proof obligations are the enclosure carrier and route,
   matching or junction data, full-field attachment and balance, and an
   intertwining transport of the frozen state registry.

The reduction rejects three semantic shortcuts: `lambda24 = 0` is not `2*pi`,
the selected stop is not automatically a spacetime edge, and positive-duration
continuation is not particle stability.
