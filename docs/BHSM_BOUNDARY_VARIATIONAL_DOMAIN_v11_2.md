# BHSM v11.2 Boundary Variational Domain

At a finite regular boundary the support variation is cancelled by Dirichlet
data, by a Neumann Legendre ensemble, or by a mixed boundary functional. The
action selects none of these. The parent Einstein-Hilbert/GHY pairing is
preserved.

The v6.7/v6.10 maximal-isotropic matter domains are sector-specific, and the
v6.15 interface trace is presymplectic-null. They do not select the support
or core domain. A complete well-posed boundary variation is not yet derived.
