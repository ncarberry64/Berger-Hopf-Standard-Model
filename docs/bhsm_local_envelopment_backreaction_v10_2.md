# BHSM v10.2 Local-Envelopment Backreaction

The M8 `chi` and `eta` sectors have explicit metric stress. The sigma, cap,
intrinsic M4, gauge, current, and matcher sectors have declared stresses or
metric responses on their own strata. They do not yet form one tensor
`T_AB,total` on a common M8 domain.

Consequently the candidates

\[
\mathcal E_\perp=T_{AB}n^An^B,
\qquad
\mathcal T_\parallel=h^{AB}T_{AB}
\]

are exact only after a common tensor owner and physical normal are selected.
The repository does not presently define the proposed compactness ratio
`localized action energy / support volume`, because neither the localized
orbit nor its gauge-invariant support is action-selected.

The v7.3 incidence audit gives

\[
\frac{\delta^2S}{\delta a_F\,\delta\Psi}=0,
\qquad
\frac{\delta^2S}{\delta a_F\,\delta H}=0
\]

for the current stratified owner. Therefore intrinsic matter does not yet
source the Hopf-radion equation through an action-derived mixed block.

Neither `d psi_star / d C_env` nor `d a_F,star / d C_env` exists, so the desired
energy-depth sign is underdetermined and was not inserted.
