# BHSM v11.1 Topological Buoyancy and Higgs Gate

Topological Buoyancy and the proposed Higgs/buoyancy identification require a
unique supported action, localized stress pullback, global restoring law,
stable branch, weak-field limit, and effective scalar representation. The
support functor is nonunique, so these quantities remain null.

No universal force, Higgs mass, vacuum expectation value, coupling, or fitted
scale is claimed.
