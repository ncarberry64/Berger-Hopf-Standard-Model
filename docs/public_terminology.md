# BHSM public terminology

Use these terms consistently across the repository and museum.

| Term | Public meaning | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Action | The proposed mathematical source whose variations are intended to own the downstream structures. | A completed physical theory. |
| Reduced action state space | The lower-dimensional mathematical state description in which the certified branch-24 event is located. | Physical spacetime or a detector event. |
| Branch-24 first stop | A certified zero/stop condition for branch 24 at the stated reduced scope. | A discovered particle or physical boundary. |
| Continuum event-child certificate | A retained mathematical certificate connecting the event-child construction to its continuum scope. | Physical encapsulation. |
| Localization/domain carrier | An action-owned covariant structure capable of carrying the reduced event into a physical spacetime/domain statement. | A plotting coordinate or chosen external container. |
| Physical encapsulation | A certified physical enclosure identified through the action-owned carrier and complete field attachment. | A reduced-state event alone. |
| Full-field attachment | Same-action attachment of the required gauge, ghost, fermion, and scalar/HS blocks and cross-derivatives. | Merely exposing software interfaces for those sectors. |
| Implemented machinery | Source, interfaces, tests, or artifacts exist. | Empirical establishment or prediction. |
| Numerically demonstrated | Behavior appears in a computation under stated numerical conditions. | An exact theorem or physical prediction without further gates. |
| Physical prediction | A physical readout frozen under the no-fit firewall after all required ownership and promotion gates close. | A provisional engine output. |
| No-fit firewall | The policy that prevents measured target values from selecting upstream branches, coefficients, normalizations, modes, or scales. | A claim that every upstream dependency is already derived. |
| Engine validation | A check of software coordinates, transformations, invariants, or execution. | Validation of BHSM as physics. |
| CMS Open Data exhibit | A checksum-pinned four-vector coordinate/engine validation. | Detector reconstruction, BHSM empirical validation, or CERN/CMS endorsement. |
| Gate 7 OPEN | At least one required promotion dependency remains unproved or unattached. | Failure of every retained mathematical result. |
| `FULL_BHSM_COMPLETE = FALSE` | The program has not met its full completion contract. | A rhetorical judgment about whether research should continue. |
