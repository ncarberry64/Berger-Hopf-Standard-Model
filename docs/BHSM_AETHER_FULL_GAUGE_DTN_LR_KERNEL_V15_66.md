# BHSM v15.66 — full-gauge DtN kernel and composite-channel weights

without introducing a factor-dependent coefficient:

\[
 S_{\rm DtN}^{SM}=\frac{K_F^{(5)}}2\int\sqrt h\left[
 \frac53 A_Y\mathcal N_TA_Y+A_2\mathcal N_TA_2+
 A_3\mathcal N_TA_3\right],

\[
 \mathcal N_T=(\Delta_1^{\rm coexact})^{1/2}.
\]

This is a nonlocal action completion derived from the rank-16 carrier trace
and the action-owned diagonal-(Sp(1)) DtN operator. It is not relabelled as a
local Yang–Mills term. Its inverse current kernels obey

\[
 G_Y:G_2:G_3=\frac35:1:1.
\]

gives, before the common chiral Fierz factor two,

\[
 C_u=\frac43+\frac35\left(\frac16\frac23\right)=\frac75,
\]

\[
 C_d=\frac43+\frac35\left(\frac16\left(-\frac13\right)\right)
 =\frac{13}{10},
\]

\[
 C_e=\frac35\left(-\frac12\right)(-1)=\frac3{10},
 \qquad C_\nu=0.
\]

Hence

\[
 K_{LR,f}(m)=2C_f\,[K_F^{(5)}\mathcal N_T(m)]^{-1}\Pi_f,
\]

and the group-theory ordering is

\[
 u>d>e>\nu.
\]

CKM or PMNS matrix. The only remaining operator in the linearized gap equation
is the left-right two-fermion susceptibility on
(\mathbb R\times S^3):

\[
 \Delta_f=2C_f(K_F^{(5)}\mathcal N_T)^{-1}

\]

computed in the actual odd-FR Weyl domain.
