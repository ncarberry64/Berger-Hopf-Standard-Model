# BHSM v11.2 Variation and Conservation

The isolated known support term yields `Box_G q_D=0`,
`T_AB=d_A q_D d_B q_D-G_AB(dq_D)^2/2`, and boundary form
`-integral sqrt(|h|) n.dq_D delta q_D`. In a completed action the support
equation would be `Box_G q_D=J_D`, but `J_D` is not action-owned until the
primitive character/current ledger exists.

Matter, wall, boundary, and core equations; the complete stress tensor;
support current; and Noether conservation identity are therefore withheld.
