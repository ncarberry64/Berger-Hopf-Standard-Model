# BHSM invariant Sobolev Schur pushforward v15.82

An individual Hessian eigenvalue and the source of its Euclidean-normalized
eigenvector depend on the chosen Galerkin coordinates.  The invariant response
is instead

\[
 \mathcal K_{LR}^{(N)}=\frac12J_{LR}^{T}\mathcal D_{KKT,N}^{-1}J_{LR}.
\]

This contraction is unchanged by any nonsingular coordinate transformation
when the Hessian and source covector are transformed together.  It was
evaluated in the nested cohomogeneity-one spaces (N=2,\ldots,8).
Although the matrices develop near-null modes, their fermionic source
projections vanish.  The full Schur response converges near magnitude (0.15)
and remains bounded along the measured event tangent.

The Einstein term is not multiplied by the eta/gauge localization weight.
The regular first-order Einstein--Cartan reduction therefore gives

\[
 G_{EC}=\frac{3}{4K_G^{(5)}}\int ds\,J|u_0|^4=0.00628089,
\]

not the off-orbit (1/W) divergence used in v15.75.  Combining the converged
Schur bound, this regular Cartan term, the common heat susceptibility, and the
same gauge DtN gives a joint gap-operator bound far below one.  Consequently
the rank-one v15.80 value of \(\delta_*\) is reclassified: the same-slice DtN
numbers remain correct, but a physical nonzero Yukawa condensate does not
follow from that coordinate-dependent one-mode inversion.

The calculation continues with the non-axisymmetric spin-stress harmonics and
their heat-regulated tail in the same invariant Schur complement.
