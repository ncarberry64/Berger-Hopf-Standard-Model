## Cosmic and local reciprocity

The Machian statement that the whole constrains the local and the local
backreacts on the whole is implemented as a dependency requirement:

1. a closed cosmic solution must select admissible background geometry and
   global topological sector;
2. local envelopment solutions must solve the coupled constraints on that
   background;
3. their complete stress must re-enter the global equations;
4. the resulting branch must remain stable under the coupled response.

The present action does not yet supply the closed cosmic solution or absolute
unit bridge. The alignment is therefore foundational, not a completed cosmic
theorem.

## Measurement

There is no external detector in the canonical ontology. An incoming state,
environment, detector, and outgoing products belong to one coupled state.
Measurement is targeted as a transition between stable or metastable basins.
Neither resonance language nor basin existence alone derives normalized
probabilities. The exact missing object is
`COUPLED_BASIN_TRANSITION_AMPLITUDE_AND_PROBABILITY_THEOREM`.
