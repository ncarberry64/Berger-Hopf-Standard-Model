The frozen ontology remains three distinct modes: core/Hopf `q_C`,
enclosure-wall/fold `q_W`, and support depth `q_D`. The seam is an incidence
locus, not a fourth deformation mode.

absent, no physical kinetic matrix, Hessian, mixed block, eigenmode, or
