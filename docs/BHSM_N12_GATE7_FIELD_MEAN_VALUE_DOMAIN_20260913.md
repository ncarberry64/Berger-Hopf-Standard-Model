# Paired endpoint field bounds and actual midpoint domain

The existing complete uniform endpoint derivatives can bound the physical
fields by integrating along each tube's radial segment. The new consumer
multiplies the signed 99-column derivative by all 75 scaled directions
before taking the interval/Euclidean group support, and retains the full
verified point-field enclosure as anchor. It selects the narrower valid
field interval in each coordinate. No new action derivatives are evaluated.

Both endpoint fields independently reproduced byte-for-byte. At endpoint 13,
83 coordinates tighten and the maximum radius decreases from approximately
4.409812395e-7 to 3.342641297e-7 (24.2 percent). At endpoint 14, 87 coordinates
tighten and the maximum radius decreases from 4.079192881e-7 to
2.819178571e-7 (30.9 percent).

A new actual Hermite-Simpson midpoint domain constructed from these paired
fields also independently reproduced. Its maximum state field-remainder
radius decreases from 2.652814163e-8 to 1.925568727e-8. Its maximum total state
hull radius decreases only from 4.486408907e-7 to 4.413684365e-7, about
1.6 percent. The descriptor hull radius decreases from 8.242945994e-13 to
4.784884367e-13. Eighty-seven coordinate hull widths tighten, with a median
new/old width ratio of approximately 0.99388.

These are width comparisons, not a proof that the new product domain is a
subset of the old one: its center can change. The new field and domain
certificates are retained, but the downstream midpoint eigenpair and full
DF campaign are deferred while a stronger local integration enclosure is
investigated. No old eigenpair proof is relabeled as a proof on the new
anchor or domain.

Eight focused tests passed, covering signed directional cancellation,
retained anchor uncertainty, complete affine groups, and existing domain
and smooth-family prerequisites. The two endpoint value pairs and the
actual midpoint domain pair have byte-identical records and numerical data.
Exact radii and source/data/receipt hashes are recorded in
`BHSM_N12_GATE7_FIELD_MEAN_VALUE_DOMAIN_20260913.json`.

The action, frozen frames, physical trial radii and empirical references
are unchanged. Full-path contraction, the physical quotient, Gate 7 and
full BHSM completion remain open. Existing Museum data and manuscript
completion claims are unchanged.
