# BHSM v15.88: same-cycle composite gap theorem

For each controlled point of the v15.86 cycle, define

\[
 G_u(t)=2\frac75\left(N_T(t)^{-1}+N_E(t)^{-1}\right)+G_{EC}(t),
\]

where the first term and the regular Einstein--Cartan overlap are derivatives
of the same localized cycle functional. The regulated zero-mass
susceptibility at radius \(R_4(t)\) is

\[
 \chi(0;t)=\frac{1}{2\pi^2R_4(t)^2}
 \sum_{n\ge0}\frac{(n+1)(n+2)}{n+3/2}
 e^{-(n+3/2)^2/R_4(t)^2}.
\]

The computed envelope and cycle average are

\[
 6.6753\times10^{-5}\leq G_u(t)\chi(0;t)
 \leq7.0359\times10^{-5},
\]

\[
 \overline{G_u\chi}=6.72468\times10^{-5}.
\]

Equivalently, the Hubbard--Stratonovich quadratic coefficient satisfies

\[
 111.821\leq G_u(t)^{-1}-\chi(0;t)\leq117.895>0.
\]

\(1=G_u(t)\chi(m;t)\) has no nonzero solution at any point of the controlled
cycle. The positive composite kinetic term cannot introduce a negative cycle
mode. Therefore

\[
 H_*=0,
 \qquad M_f^{\rm Floquet}=0,
 \qquad Y_f^{\rm cyc}=23.855948I_3\ne0.
\]

This is one result of one pushforward: the interaction vertex exists, while
the same action excludes a condensate and mass on its symmetric subcritical
cycle.
