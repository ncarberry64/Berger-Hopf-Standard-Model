# HE 0435−1223 | Chandra diffuse-emission triage

## What was found
Grant & Bautz, *Properties of galaxy groups and clusters in the fields of lensed quasars*, JAXA-SP-09-008E pp. 374–375, list HE 0435−1223 among fields with newly reported diffuse soft X-rays. Their archive cutoff was early 2009. Their method used 0.5–2 keV data, quasar subtraction, other point-source masking, exposure correction and smoothing; a thermal spectral model assumed a group or lens redshift. The report does not provide a target-specific centroid, flux, temperature, or verified emitting-group redshift. [Proceedings](https://jaxa.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/4932/files/64574000.pdf). The 11-observation [Rogers et al. reduction](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.08077v1#S2.SS1) addresses compact X-rays, not the diffuse detection or a diffuse upper limit.

## What this replay did
The eleven archival Level 2 event lists and their aspect and bad-pixel files were obtained and matched to FOV products. Primary archive payload hashes are recorded. The [Chandra Source Catalog](https://cxc.harvard.edu/csc/download/vo.html) cone returned 29 sources at 0.06 degrees in v2.2 (33 in v2.1); v2.2's four extent-flagged entries are the quasar images within 2 arcsec of the lens, not a group-size detection. This catalogue cannot set a flux upper limit for very low surface brightness gas.

The CCD7 diagnostic counts 0.5–2 keV and 3–7 keV events after excluding 15″/25″ around the quasar and 4″/6″ around catalogue sources. It compares five fixed optical centers at 21/42.052″ to geometric expectation conditional on the photon count in each 2″ lens-centered radial band in each observation. Dither-swept polygon coverage is eroded by about 5″; none of these areas is an exposure map. With the larger masks the later data give Wilson (42.052″) 720 soft counts versus a conditional geometric expectation of 687.24; the half-split excesses are about 9 and 24 counts. In the 21″ Wilson aperture the two splits have residuals of opposite sign. The more distant Sluse center has 785 vs 839.77 at 42.052″. Other tested centers do not show a consistent positive pattern. No group center was established from these counts.

This screen has **no calibrated detection significance or flux limit**. Standardized residuals use a conditional isotropic geometric null, not exposure, vignetting, particle background, unresolved sources, or PSF-wing calibration. Broad lens-centered emission is removed by conditioning on lens-centric radius. The template-retention calculation (normalized over retained geometrical pixels, nominal beta 0.5, cores 15″ or 25″) shows that the Wilson 42.052″ contrast retains only about 34–46% of a sample off-center profile; this is a diagnostic of self-subtraction, not a flux sensitivity. The historical positive is not ruled out.

## Next calibrated gate

Spatial association alone still cannot provide a measured pressure gradient or a total gravity field. The quasar image positions and time delays were not used to fit any gravity field here; the quasar sky position was used only to define the X-ray exclusion and radial control.

Run `bash acquire.sh` from the unpacked archive folder, use the official FOV files listed in `fov_sources.txt` in its parent directory, and run `python counts_triage.py` with NumPy, Matplotlib and Astropy. `exposure_maps.sh` is a prepared CIAO map entry point, not part of the completed raw triage.
