# HE 0435−1223: Chandra footprint gate and locked first diffuse screen

The eleven public 0.5–2 keV candidates are ObsIDs 7761, 11550–52, 14489–94 and 23798, from Rogers et al. [Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.08077v1#S2.SS1). 7761 and 23798 use full frames. The published exposure sum refers to the quasar, and its small cutout is not a diffuse analysis. This record contains no event or count analysis; it uses official FOV files only. CIAO/CALDB is needed for physical use of this field.

## Reproducing the geometry
- `MPLCONFIGDIR=/tmp python coverage.py` samples the official FOV polygon unions for a 42.052″ aperture (250 kpc with the adopted R1 distance) using a ~0.164″ pixel center quadrature. `MPLCONFIGDIR=/tmp python coverage.py 43.151` is a flat-distance sensitivity check. Per-CCD and union coverage, margin inside the center CCD, exact file hashes and raw LIVETIME are saved. FITS polygons sweep dither trajectories and are not bad-pixel or exposure maps. Chip overlaps can yield combined polygon containment while different CCDs contribute distinct responsive areas/times.
- [Direct CXC archive](https://cxc.cfa.harvard.edu/cdaftp/) uses `byobsid/{last-digit}/{ObsID}/primary/`; the primary directories supply `*_fov1.fits.gz`. Standard download reference: [download_chandra_obsid](https://cxc.harvard.edu/ciao/ahelp/download_chandra_obsid.html). Obtain needed primary and secondary products by observation including asol, bad pixels and mask, events and calibration as appropriate. The present gate never opened those event files.
- The extra half-chip exposures geometrically cover both main spectroscopic centers. At 14491 the outer radius crosses the CCD7 edge at the 67″ centroid; the nearby CCD6 also has field coverage there. Use the measured response, not a universal S3 response. Keep a full-frame-only reference stack to recognize detector artifacts.
- The [eROSITA catalogue cone query](https://erosita.mpe.mpg.de/erodat/apis/) with radius 0.1° on the alternate lens-group centroid returns five DR2_Main catalogue sources, no DR1_Clusters entries. The nearby quasar-position source is marked unresolved (EXT=0, EXT_LIKE=0). The other source with a nonzero extent estimate is beyond either 250 kpc test disk; catalogue results are not a diffuse flux bound.

## Account for more than one group before reading counts
Fixed search centers: Sluse nominal (69.550841°, −12.272258°), alternate (69.559414°, −12.276026°), Wilson (69.5521°, −12.2839°), and lens-centered limiting case (69.5620°, −12.2874°). Group controls: foreground z=.1744 (69.548372°, −12.280593°) and z=.4185 (69.549725°, −12.301072°); include the potentially blended z≈.562 association for background sensitivity where an instrument footprint supports it. Use physical aperture alternatives 100 and 250 kpc, radius from each branch's declared angular distance, plus broad beta-profile template alternatives (e.g. fixed 15 and 25 arcsec core scales; illustrative, not inferred). The z=.1744 projected center is only 31–42″ from the two Sluse centers. For illustration, equal 42.052″ circular apertures at those separations overlap about 54% and 39%; a diffuse excess within a simple aperture is not a redshift measurement. In contrast the .4185 and main nominal centers are about 104″ apart; broad profiles can still overlap.

1. Reprocess the event data against the same CIAO/CALDB release where possible, flag flares and confirm roll/chip status. Produce per-observation 0.5–2.0 keV, local spectrally weighted exposure maps, bad-pixel masks, and sky/particle background controls, and record effective exposed area in every aperture.
2. Before inspecting the target pixels, lock point masks using registered positions and 95–99% band-dependent PSF encircled-energy footprints. Exclude or model remaining wings from the four bright quasar images as separate variable components across observations. Also mask point sources for G1, G4 and the host of any field contaminant. Do not aggregate mismatched CCD responses into nominal live seconds.
3. Fit Poisson counts simultaneously to per-ObsID exposures and detector-dependent backgrounds, with nonnegative diffuse components for each redshift candidate and checks on how separable they are. Run the full-frame-only subset and alternate point masks as controls. Use a predeclared family of profiles and calibrate the scan and false positive rate with null and diffuse injections.
4. If projected components cannot be allocated, report a joint diffuse limit for specified templates and spectral model instead of assigning residual counts to the group. Even a real extended signal measures the projected gas distribution; multiple candidate redshifts may be unresolved and gas is not the total mass centroid.

## Verified totals from this footprint pass
- All 11 archived per-chip FOV files were parsed; both Sluse apertures and the Wilson and main-centered apertures have 100% sampled geometric coverage at both radii by union of active chip polygons. At 43.151″, 14491 alone on CCD7 covers 97.014% of the far Sluse aperture; CCD6 fills the remainder geometrically. CCD6/7 dither-swept polygons overlap, so never sum their area coverage as observations.
- FOV FITS header LIVETIME adds to 286.724 ks for the current archive, including 30.821 ks across the two full frames; this differs from the 285.15 and 29.26 ks in the published quasar reduction. The geometric ratio is roughly 9.3–9.7×, depending on which consistent accounting is used. Even using CCD7 only and the wider radius, FOV-area-weighted nominal raw livetime is 285.643 ks at the far Sluse center. None is a calibrated effective exposure or a detection.
