# Horizon drive and the loaded surface

Norman's trampoline analogy gives two independent inputs: the horizon sets the strength of the drive, and pre-existing matter and neighboring attachments set the equilibrium and compliance of the spacetime-side surface. The magnet does not denote literal electromagnetism or a spatial field in the core. For a first candidate, use independently inferred black-hole mass as a proxy for drive, not matter placed on the horizon or the ordinary black-hole potential counted twice.

Let q0 be the loaded surface equilibrium; let f(M_H) be the scalar horizon drive and s the spacetime-side collar through which it acts. After eliminating constrained variables, linearization about q0 gives H_red(q0) δq = s δf. H_red is the physical reduced Hessian, not the bare interface block. The archived gravity-loaded v0.5 seam theory genuinely contains this dependence on load through the reduced stiffness, but does not identify the horizon drive nor its light-and-stars metric transfer. In particular, the direct moving-domain term remains uncomputed. Only nonzero stable-mode eigenvalues can be inverted in this linearization.

A dimensionless sanity check: take the restoring force u+u^3. Choose three different preloads f0=u0+u0^3, with u0=0, 0.5, 1. Add equal horizon drive δf=0.1 and solve exactly by bracketed root. Equilibrium shifts are respectively 0.09903, 0.05450, 0.02454. This is a counterexample to the assumption that matching horizon masses fixes equal responses. It does not establish whether actual BHSM preloads stiffen or soften the surface. A fixed-source linear medium with fixed coefficients would show no such dependence.

First observational discriminator: predict residual lensing and stellar motions from the same response, at fixed accounted ordinary compact and extended mass. Freeze the horizon-to-drive and surface-to-metric maps before comparing lenses of similar horizon mass in different environments; vary mass at fixed environment as a separate control. HE 0435 alone cannot separate the drive from the compliance. Its calibrated optical residual is a target, not a measurement of either coefficient.
