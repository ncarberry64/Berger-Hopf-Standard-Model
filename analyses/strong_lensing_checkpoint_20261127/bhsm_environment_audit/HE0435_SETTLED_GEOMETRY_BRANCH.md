## Nonlinear coefficient needed, for an even and regular reduction

After quadratic block elimination, let q be the physical critical seam coordinate and y the remaining regular responses, in coordinates adapted to the critical direction. If there is q -> -q symmetry and the reduced action is smooth, expand a static energy-like functional under fixed boundary data:
    V - V0 = lambda*q^2/2 + beta0*q^4/4 + y^T K y/2 + q^2 h^T y + ... .
Here K is the positive complement block under the stability assumptions of this calculation. Solving y = -K^-1 h q^2 + ... gives beta_eff = beta0 - 2 h^T K^-1 h. Therefore the direct quartic alone cannot establish a lower stable branch. If lambda < 0 and beta_eff > 0 in this expansion, the two even-branch states satisfy q^2 = -lambda/beta_eff and Delta V = -lambda^2/(4 beta_eff) to leading order. The exterior metric response carried by y additionally requires its action-derived optical/time-potential projections, and direct terms in q may also contribute. The sign of Delta V does not determine the sign of either metric projection. If the physical junction has a fold instead of a smooth chart, this quartic reduction is a diagnostic only and constrained continuation on the actual sheets is required.

Next operands: identify a singularity-admitted galaxy branch domain, compute the first admissible nonlinear constrained seam term (or unfold its junction fold), and evaluate its metric projection relative to the ordinary branch. HE 0435 can then compare common-source lensing, delays and Webb dynamics without importing a halo or free coupling amplitude. No second physical equilibrium, lensing angle, or velocity prediction is claimed here.

### Outer geometry versus a smooth foreground sheet

For material differences restricted outside the inner rays, a thin spherical shell's projected fraction is 1-sqrt(1-b^2/r^2) = b^2/(2r^2) + b^4/(8r^4) + ..., where b is impact radius. The leading projected mass difference is proportional to b^2, a constant convergence across the images. The next radial orders encode outer shape. This is a local sheet approximation, not an exact mass-sheet transformation of the entire two-plane lens.

In he0435_outer_shape_vs_sheet.py, subtract an optimized constant foreground convergence from the differences between the synthetic geometries, recomputing refraction by rear G1 for each evaluation. No real data are fitted and no other model components change. At 20/50/100 kpc the largest raw pair differences 458/203/94 mas shrink to 1.464/0.128/0.016 mas. Leave-one-ray-out synthetic errors are at most 1.502/0.138/0.018 mas, allowing a common alignment on the other three. This measures how much a one-parameter smooth change can mimic the computed signal, not discriminating power in a sound data fit. Use broad radial image coverage or independent constraints on smooth convergence to look for outer shape. Time delays are a possible joint constraint with externally fixed distance or convergence, not guaranteed to break the degeneracy by themselves. There is no selected lower BHSM equilibrium or halo replacement yet.

### Which existing HE 0435 measurements help?

The synthetic fitted smooth term translates to delta kappa = -0.180, -0.080, -0.037 at return radii 20/50/100 kpc, dividing its signed mass equivalent at the Einstein radius by pi R_E^2 Sigma_crit. If a missing line-of-sight sheet were proposed to undo that reduction relative to a frozen baseline, it would need the opposite (positive) shift. The count-based external-convergence distribution used by Wong has median +0.003 and central 68% interval [-0.016,+0.034], with nearby perturbers handled explicitly rather than twice. The independent Tihhonova weak-lensing MSE map gives -0.012 with 16/84% quantile offsets -0.013/+0.020. In the 50 kpc trial the +0.080 shift is substantially larger than either quoted central range around its median. These are comparisons of scales, not a formal exclusion or a significance calculation. Published external convergence describes environment and line-of-sight contributions with different apertures and assumptions. The synthetic term is a lens-plane equivalent that can also be carried by the internal radial profile or traded with source scale. Do not identify the two observables or multiply the published distributions as independent measurements of a local envelope.

Use Wong's extended HST host arcs to constrain shape within the observed radial coverage, with independent optical PSF modeling and a shared reconstruction within each band. They are one extended host, not multiple source redshifts. Use the independent environmental distributions to constrain a physical external sheet; use Webb resolved stellar velocities and the published time delays alongside a declared distance/foreground prior. Time delays are not an automatic mass-sheet breaker. A fair next paper test starts with a conventional composite lens that fits the arcs and is forward-modeled through the same tracer and observational masks, then substitutes an independently derived BHSM equilibrium instead of inserting the synthetic shell.
