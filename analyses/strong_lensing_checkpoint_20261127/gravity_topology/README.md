# HE 0435−1223: source-led gravity topology
No seam, tide-equality surface, source-position fit, fitted halo, or time-delay calibration is used here. The projection is the observed-plane lensing terrain (not a unique 3-D reconstruction). The associated group is fixed by Sluse et al. (https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.00382), with an assumed cored finite-mass profile and nominal normalization. The foreground lens galaxy uses the prior circularized, core-regularized light-to-stellar-mass proxy; the rear G1 has fixed stellar photometry and is traced in its own plane. The images come from Courbin et al. (https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.1473). This map omits an independent general line-of-sight mass field and is not a complete lens mass reconstruction.

Three identical-source two-screen comparisons vary only the associated group: absent, its exact projected field, and the second-order Taylor expansion of its projected optical potential about the lens center (constant deflection plus a constant convergence/shear matrix, no fit). The latter is the local broad-background control, not a literal flat potential. The four-ray comparison subtracts rigid source translations. The group is appreciable but its departure from that smooth approximation is small in this proxy, and does not close the common-source gap. The prior single-domain R1 solve is read back against its own fixed baseline, not identified as the complete BHSM environmental-displacement action.

The replay checks analytical ray-map Jacobians by finite differences, checks stellar projection sampling at two resolutions and reproduces the independent archived ordinary ray coordinates. It leaves physical delays unreported because all four sightlines must first close on one physical source. Next test: apply the same frozen terrain readouts to independently constrained additional sources and to a separately specified BHSM displacement map. Identical effective fields imply identical lensing. The paper's additional science must come from an independently predicted and testable field property, not relabeling these contours.

Run from this folder: `MPLCONFIGDIR=/tmp/bhsm-mpl OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python topology.py`. Inputs and R1 comparison live in the adjacent bhsm_environment_audit folder. The PNG maps the projected group and foreground curvature and plots the rear-plane-traced source scatter; the regional map is not a measurement of any absolute potential level.
