# Fixed-mass spin-energy sensitivity of the settled well

Test Norman's proposed spin energy-to-core-coupling channel without counting the rotational energy as another ordinary gravitating mass. Compare at fixed compact total mass and identical stellar mass map, ordinary light propagation and distances. The rotational energy fraction e=1-sqrt[(1+sqrt(1-chi²))/2] is the Kerr normalization only; the BHSM coupling of that number to the surrounding field is unknown. The following unit-response laws are independently declared examples, not derived or fitted spin laws. No direct Kerr spin term or moving-pin wake is added.

Freeze the earlier Chabrier distributed full-shape solution at ell=52.81 kpc and projected source weights p,s. For A=τ(-Delta)+D Delta², ell²=D/τ and the response amplitudes for fixed forcing are inversely proportional to τ. Use the full squared-gradient light response (including the cross term). At spin magnitudes 0, 0.5, 0.9, 1 evaluate four maps: multiply the optical shape by 1+e; multiply only the pin force by 1+e; multiply D by 1+e at fixed τ and sources; or reduce τ by 1/(1+e) at fixed D and sources. The last case increases both reach and compliance. All four laws are spin-direction invariant, as required by a scalar energy hypothesis. They are not models of the signed kinematic wake.

Equalizing the first aperture is a separate amplitude-removal diagnostic, not recalibration to both apertures. It keeps a 1.07% shift in the second-aperture ratio in the latter two arms. Refits use only shear and the S1 nuisance with the 14 early-source knots. The nonspinning baseline closes the two aperture targets and has 31.4 mas RMS on the early knots. For the raw softer-tension arm, B superficially improves to 184 mas, but the excess has risen in both apertures and early-knot RMS doubles to 66.3 mas. After equalizing the first aperture the early RMS returns to 31.5 mas and B is still off by 314 mas. No spin or law is selected using B; its position is used only at scoring, with A as the seed source datum. For the fixed-τ reach-only arm the raw root test does not return a valid B, not a proof of no counterimages. The conventional matched Chabrier reference is about 38 mas.

Result: stronger spin energy can make the well both stronger and broader in this explicitly assumed softening model, but the examples do not simultaneously preserve the inner calibration and predict B. An action-owned sensitivity of the regular surface or optical map to spin energy remains necessary; the Kerr partition itself gives neither its sign nor magnitude. No transfer to HE 0435.
