# Can central initiation communicate outward and leave inward transport?

This is a causal regular-spacetime-side control of Norman's initiation picture, not a derived BHSM material model. Retain the coupling as a persistent interface; temporarily turning the imposed *driving differential* off in a control does not remove the interface or introduce a core clock.

On an 18 kpc proxy interval, let q be the perturbation of stock per radial length (the geometrical area factor has been absorbed in the variable) and F signed radial flux, positive outward. Solve q_t+F_x=0 and F_t+c_s² q_x=-γ(x)F. The characteristic speeds are ±c_s, so a signal can reach a larger radius even while the flux behind it is inward. Time is in units of kpc/c_s, with c_s=1 in the calculation, and stock/flux units are arbitrary. Use the nominal ring scales 5.149 and 7.630 kpc for a resistance band, with damping γ=.35 times [1+3 f(x)] and f the smooth unit-peak bump spanning them; compare no band. Boundary drive sets q=-1 at the central regular boundary, with q=0 at an outer reservoir. Other controls use a pulse of duration 8, a sealed outer edge, or no drive, with all interiors initially at rest and the same positive stock reference.

The steady open solution has constant F=-c_s²/∫γ dx for this unit depth difference. If either the depth difference dies away or the outer edge is sealed, the steady flux is zero. A sealed model can settle into a different stock while F vanishes, unlike the pulse which relaxes back to background in this open model. The energy identity ∂t[(c_s²q²+F²)/2]+c_s²∂x(qF)=-γF² explicitly says sustained transport is supported by the boundary drive, not by free energy from a past start.

At 720 cells, the saved dimensionless inward flux settles to -0.1301 at both rings, versus -0.1587 without the band (18% less throughput). The pulse and sealed controls decay to below 3e-7 in the same units at their late checkpoints; the no-drive case is identically zero. Numerical flux threshold crossings occur in order, at about 5.08 and 8.68; these are threshold times with finite-resolution smearing, not the strict characteristic front times R/c_s or astrophysical ages. The perturbed system has positive stock everywhere when added to the declared background. Global stock changes agree with integrated boundary flux in each run to <3e-10 and two grid sizes agree with analytic steady flux within 2e-4.

This shows a viable distinction in a trial equation: the disturbance can travel outward and initiate inward transport, but a transient push by itself does not give permanent flow in the dissipative model. Persistent coupling and available supply would both matter. Increased band resistance steepens the local stock gradient and lowers throughflow; it does not automatically produce an absolute stock increase above the original background. Unlike the earlier imposed-throughput lens test, the held-pressure experiment lets throughput change. There is no assigned BHSM relation from q or F to the nonconformal metric. No physical c_s, boundary strength, shape, held-out image calculation or transfer is claimed.
