# Centered moving-wake control and physical-input check

A homogeneous filter with one exponential response time is a useful exact null for the moving-pin calculation. In one spatial Fourier mode, let x=k·vτ and p=exp(-t_stop/τ). Before stopping, the response is a weighted trail of translations. After stopping, weight 1-p sits on the current pin and p retains the approach integral. With each pin response shifted back by its own centroid, the transfer for the two signs is exp(∓i p x)[1-p+p/(1∓i x)]. The absolute odd half-difference begins at (p-p²+p³/3)|x|³, not linear order. At |x|=.3 it is 0.818%, 0.616%, and 0.117% of that *static spatial mode* at rest times 0, 1τ, and 3τ, respectively. This is not a percent of Jackpot's lensing.

Verified the transfer against direct exponential history integration and put the centered filter into the existing three-dimensional quadratic snapshot solver. Halving drift across four steps produces the expected factor of about eight in the nondegenerate odd residual, even with the same fixed host cross term and nuisance-removal machinery. The earlier trial uses a scale-dependent memory τ(k) proportional to 1/[k²(1+k²)], so its nuisance-cleaned odd response instead halves. The latter is not a violation of the null: its Fourier phase contains the k-dependent coefficient exp[-A(k)t]/A(k), which cannot be removed by one spatial translation. This does not validate that dependence for BHSM or alter the frozen Jackpot transfer result.

Independent source check: [Turner et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08771#S5.SS3) separate compact mass excess from the smaller black-hole scaling proxy; their stellar dispersion is not a black-hole trajectory. [Ballard et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.04535#S5.SS2.SSS3) fit the off-center point-mass alternative to all three sources, so it cannot be imported to predict source 3 as a holdout and yields no measured motion vector. Compare against ordinary collisionless response on the same orbit and stellar phase-space inputs if those inputs become available ([Tremaine & Weinberg](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/209/4/729/955398)). Neither the uniform filter nor the scale-dependent trial is a physical calibration. No new Jackpot ray fit or forecast.
