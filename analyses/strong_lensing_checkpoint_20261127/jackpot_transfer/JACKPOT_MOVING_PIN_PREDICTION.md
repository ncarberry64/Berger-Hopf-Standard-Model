# Trial wake propagated through the Jackpot lens

This is a sensitivity forecast, not a physical estimate of any Jackpot orbit or BHSM wake. It asks whether the previously fixed stopped-trajectory odd snapshot is absorbed by the early-ring lens fit and whether it can meaningfully change the outer image at a declared normalization.

Use the previous static Chabrier full-shape baseline selected solely by its inner slope and two nominal apertures. It predicts a B error of 346 mas. The ordinary stellar/compact contribution is unchanged. Add only the odd half-difference of the prior paired histories (dimensionless opposite drifts 0.24, both at rest for 0.10). Insert it as a signed extra main-plane deflection; do not add the trial model's radial monopole or its even motion response. The template uses the prior trial force weights and Salpeter stellar profile proportions; the lens's independently preserved Chabrier masses are not changed. To define a nonphysical but reproducible angular sensitivity scale, match the RMS of the *trial static* shape deflections to the RMS of the *calibrated static excess* at the 14 early knots. Rotation changes this scale negligibly. This is not an inferred trajectory, timed coefficient, or measured amplitude.

Test eight directed rotations in 45-degree steps with no selection by the held-out image. Recalibrate only the same two shear coefficients and S1 deflector strength on the first two sources' 14 knots; the circular shape/known masses remain frozen. For each arm, back-project measured A and solve for its other image using the same initial seed as the baseline, then compare to observed B. This is local counterimage tracing rather than an all-image census. The nuisance fit does not absorb all of the wake. At the fine grid the largest image movement is about 70 mas. Across the eight prescribed directions the B error is 290–410 mas, while inner-knot RMS is 30.4–39.2 mas (baseline 31.4 mas). The direction with the lowest inner RMS gives approximately 410 mas B error. The direction with minimum evaluated B error makes the early RMS worse, about 39.2 mas. Neither extremum is a fitted or inferred direction. The conventional matched Chabrier control remains near 38 mas.

The result is reproduced on two grids/box sizes; the zero-wake map reproduces the saved static B position. No transfer to HE 0435 and no claimed rejection of a true wake. This is the result at one prescribed trial strength, snapshot optics, finite direction set and chosen baseline. To make the physical forecast without trading on B, the motion history and regular causal surface/metric response must be independently determined.
