# Jackpot calibration target for the settled-envelope hypothesis

Use SDSS J0946+1006 to calibrate candidate geometry; do not tune on HE 0435. Carry earlier HE 0435 results only as a later cross-system check, not as a literally blind dataset. The singularity is the candidate condition for the equilibrium, not an added central mass or a pumping/strain term. This calibration is effective projected geometry, not a selected BHSM equilibrium.

Turner, Smith & Collett (2024, doi:10.1093/mnras/stae263) impose 3.08e11 solar masses inside the 1.397 arcsec inner Einstein aperture and report main-lens reduced deflection 1.89 +/- 0.03 arcsec at the 2.07 arcsec outer ring, for the second source redshift. This is model-derived from the same joint reconstruction as their MUSE slope, not a raw independent second mass. The full ring is 2.07 arcsec partly because the first source is also a lens; its reported ~0.16 arcsec contribution is not put into the main galaxy's mass. Convert the outer deflection via M2/M1 = (theta2/theta1)(alpha2/theta1)(w1/w2), w=D_ls/D_s, using the fixed archived R1 weight ratio 0.71849. Thus the normalized two-aperture target is 3.08e11 and 4.44e11 Msun at nominal 5.15 and 7.63 kpc. Distances and convention are stated explicitly: use Turner's published mass to anchor scale and R1 ratios to transfer it. This is not a re-fit of their axisymmetric light/Jeans or multi-plane data.

Hold the independent Sonnenfeld photometric components frozen from s3_stellar_transfer.py; use their sphericalized projected tPIEMD profiles, with Chabrier and Salpeter as separate illustrative normalizations, not confidence endpoints. Subtraction gives equivalent excess (2.15,3.31)e11 Msun for Chabrier or (1.35,2.36)e11 Msun for Salpeter. The ordinary compact and environmental terms have not been separately subtracted in this simplified two-aperture control; those contributions are not identified as BHSM by subtraction. In a full control use the independently inferred components once.

A power-law through just the two points fits by construction. Its implied lens-equivalent total slope (computed from the deflection curvature, not by equating it to the local density derivative) is 1.12 or 1.24, versus Turner's 0.96 +/- 0.02 free-halo value, also 0.93 +/- 0.02 for their NFW-like set. This comparison is diagnostic and correlated, not a significance claim. The mismatch shows that two-point excess amount is insufficient to select geometry, even before the raw stellar dispersion map is scored. The account must connect amplitude and curvature across sites, not transplant a universal scalar excess fraction or a power law. Turner MUSE map, outer ring with source-plane lens propagation, and third-source astrometry should be scored as observables under the same potentials with independent stellar normalization and declared outer conditions.

Replay python jackpot_equilibrium_calibration.py. Save the resulting constraints as a target before deriving a BHSM branch; no new free parameter was imported into BHSM and no lens data were fit beyond the two-point effective power comparison. HE 0435 has not been adjusted.
