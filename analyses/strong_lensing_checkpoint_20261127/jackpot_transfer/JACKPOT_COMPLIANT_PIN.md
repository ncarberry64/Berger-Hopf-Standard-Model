# Jackpot compliant pin: a loaded equilibrium, not an imposed depression

This is a conditional calculation motivated by Norman's trampoline analogy. The ordinary stellar and compact profiles are held fixed. In a flat spherical static scalar reduction let u be depth, A=-Delta+ell² Delta² the archived positive bulk operator, j the common ordinary load, and C_s the average over a spherical interface of radius s. Compare the minimizers of E0=1/2 <u,A u>-<j,u> and E1=E0+kappa/2 (C_s u-d)^2 at fixed finite positive stiffness. Here d is a preferred core-side value, not the actual depth, and is not yet derived. The calculation assigns no additional ordinary mass to the black hole. Where general relativity is already used to trace the ordinary lens, the difference of the two toy depth fields (not either absolute toy field) is read as an additional equal-potentials optical field. This last map remains assumed.

Stationarity gives u0=A^-1 j and u1=u0+q A^-1 C_s^*, where q=kappa(d-C_s u0)/(1+kappa K_ss) and K_ss=C_s A^-1 C_s^*>0. This solves both depth and reaction, with a positive coupled quadratic Hessian. At the coupled functional, u1 lowers energy relative to evaluating that same functional at u0. It does not infer physical energy across different actions or the true BHSM branch. For multiple pins the simultaneous reaction equation is (K+diag(1/kappa_i))q=d-C u0; each pin changes the reaction at all others.

**Shape limitation:** for fixed A, a pin whose energy depends on only one scalar trace produces one spatial response pattern, even for a nonlinear pin energy (only q changes). A nonlinear local force law by itself does not widen/change the normalized shape of a point Green function. At a point the lensing-equivalent excess is the same q[1-xK1(x)] profile as the earlier fixed-depth control after amplitude fitting. To change shape in this model, the core coupling must change where the membrane responds, add independent trace channels, modify the surrounding operator, or change the metric readout. These are alternative physical hypotheses, not interchangeable knobs.

For a positive source on a finite shell, outside the shell the optical excess is q[1-sinh(s/ell)/(s/ell) xK1(x)] with x=R/ell. This follows from the Green shell average and agrees with independent line-of-sight force integrals. The trace in the uncoupled state is independently obtained by integrating the same operator against the stellar density and fixed point proxy. For each proposed extent, infer ell and one reaction normalization from the two inner Salpeter aperture residuals; at fixed stiffness, this calibrates d, then the code solves forward for u1 and q again. Varying dimensionless stiffness between .25, 1 and 4 changes the inferred preferred value but gives the same calibrated reaction and optical shape. The ring shifts constrain net response, not the strength/depth pair independently. Geometry nuisance fits use only the first two rings. No S3 data entered the calibration, interface radii or continuation choices.
