# Ordinary frame dragging versus the Jackpot wake

Compare the spin-only term from a stationary Kerr black hole to the normalized moving-entrance wake. At leading weak-field order its deflection equals that of the same compact Schwarzschild term shifted transversely by a spin-dependent vector of norm at most |a|=|J|/(Mc)≤GM/c². The ordinary mass itself is not added or rescaled. The leading direct angular bend is bounded by 4G|J|/(c³ b²)=4(r_g/b)² at extremal spin and favorable orientation; the leading approximation is degenerate with a compact mass center offset, not with an arbitrary scale-dependent wake. Higher orders break that degeneracy but carry additional powers of r_g/b in this isolated Kerr limit. Source: [Asada, Kasai & Yamamoto](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0301099); weak-deflection Kerr context [Iyer & Hansen](https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.0085).

The four test impact radii for the central source are about 5.15, 7.63, 13.11 and 4.97 kpc, using the shared archived angular conversion. The nominal ordinary central compact proxy is 1.6e9 solar masses, not a measured horizon mass. A stress comparison attributes the entire approximately 8e9 solar mass compact excess to an extremal spin when estimating the maximum spin term, though [Turner et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08771#S5.SS3) distinguish that excess from the smaller BH estimate. Rescale only the spin coefficient in this stress limit; no refit with a new ordinary mass component is implied.

In the same frozen Chabrier shape baseline as the moving-wake test, propagate the two transverse spin basis components through the first-two-source refit and solve B from A. Central differences with artificial translation steps of 0.5 and 1 mas agree; multiply the resulting linear response by the actual spin displacement afterward. The largest singular value maximizes over all projected spin directions without selecting one using B. Nominal black hole proxy gives direct unreduced bending ≤1.96e-7 mas and image movement ≤4.70e-7 mas in this model. The high-mass stress gives direct unreduced bending ≤4.90e-6 mas and image movement ≤1.18e-5 mas including the early-ring refit. Compare the latter, 0.012 microarcseconds, to 70 mas for the earlier trial wake. The extreme spin estimate is for the modeled central object; it does not bound an unknown different object arbitrarily near a ray.

This excludes direct stationary Kerr spin as the explanation of the earlier trial-scale signal at the tested impacts. Reversing prior orbital direction at fixed present position, velocity and spin leaves stationary Kerr unchanged; reversing the present spin reverses the leading spin deflection. A BHSM collective response driven by spin, or an ordinary matter wake with its own environmental history, is a separate mechanism and needs a sourced transfer to the optical field. The calculation does not model either or infer a spin or orbit from the third source.
