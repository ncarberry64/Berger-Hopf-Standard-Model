## Dust loading and optical readout

Consider the minimal induced-metric bridge on a regular timelike hypersurface, with ordinary matter and photons minimally coupled to the same induced metric. Fix the bulk metric for this particular variation and take outward normal displacement w. The archive supplies delta_w gamma_ab=2 w K_ab. In the conventional stress definition delta S_m=(1/2) integral sqrt(-gamma) T^{ab} delta gamma_ab, the normal forcing is T^{ab} K_ab (the orientation or energy convention may reverse its overall sign). Nonrelativistic matter with T^{ab}=rho c² u^a u^b supplies rho c² K(u,u).

For a background null ray k normalized in a local screen, the metric contribution to first-order light ray optics is (1/2) delta gamma(k,k)=w K(k,k). At fixed endpoints in a static localized flat-patch Born arm, variation of the ray direction on the screen is the transverse gradient of the integral of this contraction, with a sign if deflection is defined as the opposite of the outgoing direction change. Let d be the dimension of the regular worldvolume and K_ab=(tr K/d)gamma_ab+Khat_ab. The trace term drops out identically for photons. If Khat=0, the metric change is purely conformal and gives no extra first-order change of null paths, irrespective of the spatial shape of w. The same displacement couples to massive matter: K(u,u)=-(tr K)/d for unit timelike u. This is consistent with the standard conformal invariance of unparametrized null geodesics ([Gourgoulhon, Geometry and Physics of Black Holes](https://relativite.obspm.fr/blackholes/bholes.pdf)). This does not claim that all endpoint distance, clock or redshift observables are conformally invariant. Nor does it neglect the ordinary lens off of which one would linearize.

This rules out using the archived spherical umbilic seam matching condition alone as a direct linear optical bridge. Its contraction defining the threshold is not the contraction the photon samples. An anisotropic extrinsic geometry can give nonzero Khat(k,k), and physical metric backreaction delta_bulk gamma(k,k) could also supply bending when the direct displacement term vanishes. A nonzero local contraction is necessary for the isolated direct channel, not sufficient for a Jackpot image shift: the transverse derivative and line integral can still cancel or reduce to existing nuisance templates.

## Feeding the differential scalar/topographic mode

A formal load from ordinary matter enters through the regular geometry in this bridge, not directly as an arbitrarily chosen v5.7 difference-mode force. With x=(T,Phi_internal), choose a physical shape solution w and take the mixed derivative S_xw on the same domain. Then the source of the difference mode is proportional to e_-^T S_xw[w]. If one only multiplies the entire already-stationary reduced potential by a common geometry-dependent prefactor, its fixed-profile mixed derivative vanishes at equilibrium: d_w d_x (a(w)V(x))=a'(w) grad V(x0)=0. This is a control, not a claim that each local density in the actual archive is separately stationary.

In the v5.6 collar term integral B_threshold J, the separate contribution from the Jacobian is the depth integral of (delta_w J) (D_x B_threshold·e_-), with additional terms from varying the kernel, embedding, domain, and profile. At a parallel flat-ambient umbilic patch in Gaussian normal coordinates with fixed collar endpoints J=(1+kappa rho)^d; constant normal shift gives a depth weight delta_w J/J=d kappa w/(1+kappa rho). Cancellation of an unweighted difference-mode integral therefore does not imply cancellation of the displacement moment. This identifies a specific piece to recover from the physical nonhomogeneous profile, without treating the illustrative umbilic Jacobian as the actual galaxy. The archived normalized J=1 throws this depth dependence away; it cannot determine the sign or magnitude of the difference-mode load. Even a nonzero difference-mode load still needs the optical bridge above or an independently derived nonconformal metric response.
