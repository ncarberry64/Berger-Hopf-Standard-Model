# Jackpot: quadratic bending from regular surface displacement

The earlier result excluded first-order bending from trace-only displacement of a fixed ambient umbilic hypersurface. The [archive's normal-shape action](https://github.com/ncarberry64/Berger-Hopf-Standard-Model/blob/551ce2c8e4e672538e520629b0d4a8962cbba968/docs/bhsm_full_geometric_gauge_fixed_hessian_v5_11.md) also supplies a flat spatial shape operator as a conditional control. For a static graph at zero background extrinsic curvature, the area term contributes a gradient quadratic, and the squared trace/shape terms contribute squared Laplacian/Hessian quadratics. On a decaying flat patch their integrated quadratic forms give the principal operator A=τ(-Delta)+D Delta² with D=2(c_K2+c_S) for the displayed density convention; assume positive coefficients of the principal operator. This does not assign observed physical values, include the full collar, choose its boundary conditions, or identify an ambient geometry for Jackpot.

## First nonconformal graph term

On the flat fixed-ambient graph, the induced metric is exactly gamma=eta+dw dw. On a conformally umbilic reference in constant-curvature ambient space the parallel-surface factor is conformal and the derivative term gives the same leading local nonconformal result about w=0. This is a fixed-background, zero-reference-gradient comparison, with the ordinary lens map supplied separately. It is not the general on/off difference about an already deformed surface: if the reference displacement is w0, the cross terms between dw0 and the additional displacement must also be included. We do not compute bulk metric reaction.

For a spherical static w(r), the extra coordinate optical path length in the Born control is
D(R)=∫_R^∞ w'(r)^2 sqrt(r²-R²)/r dr.
Differentiate in R: the inward deflection magnitude is -D'(R)=∫_0^(pi/2) [w'(R sec theta)]² dtheta. Equivalent projected mass is c² R[-D'(R)]/(4G). This is a null readout of surface gradients, not a separately added mass halo. It is quadratic in graph slope and independent of the sign of w within the stated zero-gradient control. Projected curvature can still be weak, decline at large impact parameter, or become inconsistent with the lens.

## Exact obstruction for the normal point Green response

The positive local Green profile is w=Q(1-exp(-r/ell))/r, ell²=D/τ, with the same operator form as the archived static reduction. For Q>0, -w'(r)=Q∫_0^(1/ell) t exp(-rt)dt is strictly decreasing. Hence M_eq(R)/R strictly decreases. At Jackpot's first two nominal aperture radii this bounds the optical mass ratio by R1/R0=1.482, for every ell and Q. The nominal required inferred excess ratio is 1.750 for Salpeter or 1.544 for Chabrier after subtracting the fixed ordinary model. This rejects the quadratic point-response shortcut for these two nominal aperture summaries. It is not a bound on arbitrary positive extended sources, mixed reference terms, or bulk/metric backreaction; no joint observational covariance was propagated.

## Broad smooth comparison, then a held-out ray

The fixed Gaussian shape w=A exp(-r²/(2 L²)), not selected by the archived action, yields M_eq(R)=c² A² sqrt(pi) R² exp(-R²/L²)/(8 G L³). The two aperture residuals set L and A analytically. Nominal Salpeter gives L=11.82 kpc, A=46.6 pc, maximum slope .00239, and a total inner equivalent slope 0.949 (published model benchmark [0.96 ± 0.02](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.08771)). Fit only the three nuisances with the early 14 knots and forecast B from A without using B in the fit. The nominal Salpeter forecast misses B by 673 mas, compared with about 97 mas for the matched conventional control. Chabrier fits the apertures but gives slope 0.104 and the fixed counterimage solver does not return an admissible root; this is not a proof of no counterimage globally. A decaying Gaussian is also not obtained from the positive point source. For constant coefficients its required load integrates to zero because A is a sum of divergences, so a nonzero Gaussian load cannot have a single sign over the flat domain unless support or matching outside the patch changes the bookkeeping.

Projection was independently verified against line integration and optical-delay differentiation. Point ratio bound verified on 101 length scales in addition to the exact monotonic argument. Replay is byte-identical: OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python jackpot_quadratic_embedding.py.

Conclusion: the second-order geometric channel exists, but both the point shortcut and the tested broad Gaussian fail to give a viable Jackpot prediction. The next useful calculation is the nonconformal metric response of an action-selected distributed load around the already settled reference, including the collar and bulk variation.
