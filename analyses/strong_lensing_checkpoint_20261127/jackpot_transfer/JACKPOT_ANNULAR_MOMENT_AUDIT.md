# Inter-ring flow: distinguish hidden inner tide from a broad wake

Audit the prior flow calculation without refitting source 3. For nonnegative projected mass restricted to the annulus a≤r≤b, let dQ2 be its complex second angular moment, I=∫r^-2 dQ2 and O=∫r² dQ2. The two moments determine the direct quadrupole inside and outside respectively. Since |dQ2|≤dM and |r²-a²b²/r²|≤b²-a², the exact inequality is |O-a²b²I|≤M(b²-a²). The R1 geometry used for the frozen lens gives reduced source-3 direct-bending limits at A of 51.2 and 59.3 mas (Salpeter and Chabrier) for I=0 with the full nominal excess budget. The distinct flat-distance Dreamer estimate was 49–57 mas. Saturation requires independently variable radial mass and arbitrarily concentrated angular mass. The conditions exclude signed effective distributions and nonlocal optical responses.

A stronger test keeps the previous Chabrier radial mass profile fixed, as required for the same ordinary/excess accounting. The supremum of the outer moment for I=0 then is ∫ dM |r²-r_c^4/r²|, with r_c the median of the measure dM/r². This follows by subtracting λ I and taking λ=r_c^4; choosing dQ2=-dM below r_c and +dM above it realizes the dual bound as angular concentration becomes pointlike. Here r_c=1.6643 arcsec and the direct A deflection is at most 29.0 mas. For an added positive component with only an azimuthal monopole and sinusoidal quadrupole, |dQ2|≤dM/2, lowering the bound to 14.5 mas. These are bounds on one direct reduced quadrupolar bend, not on the image position or any other harmonic. No assumption of a detected sign flip.

The actual earlier coherent slowdown arm has nonzero I, with direct reduced quadrupole bends of 52.3 mas at observed B and 7.67 mas at observed A. Thus the earlier 67 mas improvement in the held-out image cannot be read as a 67 mas outer-ray tide; the affected inner-side ray and the nuisance refit matter too. This arm is not subject to an I=0 bound.
