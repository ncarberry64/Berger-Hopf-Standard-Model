# Data-led direction and strength: ordinary tide control

Setup: compute the trace-free part of the projected deflection derivative
at the main lens, from neighboring galaxies rather than the lensed arcs.
Hold its tensor fixed, and calibrate only the round main deflection
amplitude and slope from the same development sets as the cross-lens
mode control. These are source-plane angular closure differences, not
matched image-plane centroids.

* HE 0435: Sluse et al. group centroid, dispersion 470 ±100 km/s,
  retained spherical truncated group proxy, and explicit G1 on its
  separate redshift plane (fixed stellar model). Ordinary group
  stretch at quasar redshift 0.0255 nominal or 0.0387 in the nearer
  centroid variant. Held D vs A falls from 0.0521 arcsec to 0.0217
  or 0.0200 arcsec; all centroid/velocity scenarios retain a nonzero
  gap (0.0176–0.0316 arcsec). Compare only with same fixed G1 control.
* CSWA 31: outer catalog members only, masses proportional to their
  aperture light. Use central SED stellar mass 1.6e12 solar masses
  as scale; measured circular median-annular light from 8–10 arcsec
  turns into 3329–3639 electrons/s after outer 15–22 arcsec sky
  subtraction. This is a common M/L stellar proxy, not total mass.
  Stand-in Plummer sizes based on apparent half-aperture-light.
  Aperture-light and angular-cut controls imply reduced stretch
  0.00019–0.00174. S5 improves from 2.564 to 2.489 arcsec
  at most; S4 changes by under 0.005 arcsec.
* Jackpot: put possible SW companion on the main plane at measured
  approximate separation 40 arcsec, native optical frame oriented
  using Gavazzi et al. Fig. 1; sweep PA 210/225/240 and mass
  1e11/1e12 solar masses as hypothetical controls, no secure
  companion mass or distance assumed. For the higher mass the reduced
  inner stretch is ~0.00395. Outer S2 closure shifts from 0.711 to
  0.702–0.709 arcsec. It does not establish any fixed physical
  amplitude. S3 remains outside this coordinate-frame replay.

This is a known optical effect, not a native BHSM contribution.
The lens environment data supply direction and relative scale only
within the stated model. Flat/azimuthal stretch is a low-order
projection, not a full foreground assignment. CASSOWARY and Jackpot
leave out later deflectors here, so thresholds are comparative only.
A collective-BHSM test must add an action-derived shared-field
response without double-counting direct propagation.
