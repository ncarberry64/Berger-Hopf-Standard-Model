# SNELLS light-only controls

No foreground mass, halo, luminosity-to-mass conversion, dark fraction, or kinematics were used in the image measurements. Sky positions of the fields and published image identifications were used. Neither test is BHSM validation; neither demonstrates that a lens is dark-matter-free.

## Measurements on calibrated HST SCI cutouts

- SNL-1 and SNL-2: MAST WFC3 program [14210](https://www.stsci.edu/hst-program-info/program/?program=14210). UVIS F336W + F814W for SNL-1 and F390W + F814W for SNL-2. Using sky-seeded searches and local low-order foreground *light* subtraction, SNL-1's UV A/B separation is ~4.89 arcsec over four background prescriptions, compared with published 4.86 +/- 0.06 arcsec (full separation). The same B is weak in the simple red residuals near the foreground galaxy. UV A/B changes from ~1.5–1.7 at 0.12 arcsec to ~2.2–4.0 at 0.32 arcsec under the three quadratic prescriptions (the plane fit is worse). Neither same-radius aperture comparison nor peak significance is a matched-source or PSF-aware measurement; no pass claimed.
- SNL-2's UV local maxima are near the published 4.60 arcsec pair separation (~4.58 arcsec in this exploratory search). B is weak (~0.5–1.4 times the robust per-pixel background-residual scale for a smoothed peak; not a formal S/N), and aperture B flux can change sign. Not a credible two-band morphology/flux control. See [Collier et al. Fig. 1 and Table 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.01931#page=3).
- SNL-0 (ESO 325–G004): MAST ACS program 10710 visit 02 aligned F475W and F625W products. In approximate arc windows identified in [Smith et al. Fig. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0504453#page=3), resampled sky at 50 mas/pixel, difference-of-Gaussians with narrow 1 pixel and broad 3–5 pixel; local independent ridge maximization along 18 radial cuts across bright arc A gives RMS color-to-color radial shift 22–26 mas. This is a descriptive same-arc comparison within one HST pixel; not formal precision, independent samples, or a lens-model constraint. Shorter B/C are less stable (49–109 and 82–174 mas respectively, approximate depending on filter). No neutral-matter or BHSM interpretation at this stage; [independent GR benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08300).

Replay scripts: snl_light_audit.py (calibrated SCI extraction only), snl_light_measure.py (nearby pair sensitivity), snl0_arc_shape.py (cross-band arc ridge). The archive inventory and public product IDs are in snl_inputs/dl.list; download through MAST API v0.1 Download/file. Some very large archive FITS have only the required validated SCI byte strips downloaded locally, not whole calibrated files; use complete products or verified SCI data for reproduction. No repository or manuscript changes. Next non-circular geometry test: identify replicated landmarks across two images of the same background patch (not 18 points on one arc), freeze the warp on at least three noncollinear pairs, and test unused structure in both colors.

## Cross-copy check: candidate mirror arms of arc A

The discovery image labeled A looks like a plausible two-image fold (Smith et al., 2005); the correspondences are a hypothesis, not input from a mass model. The brightest peak on each arm in F475W fixes the equal-scale reflection. Holding out the interior before testing it in both bands, the F625W bright peaks match the frozen angular reflection within about 0.048 arcsec along the arc. The interior brightness profile correlation is -0.027 (blue) / +0.502 (red); 27 nearby extractions using the same anchor map give blue -0.178 to +0.199, red +0.369 to +0.661. This is not a robust across-band prediction of interior structure. The simple reflection may be inadequate for a real fold; unresolved differences, ordinary lens stretch or an uncertain feature correspondence are not new physics. See snl0_fold_holdout.py and its JSON for the stretch control calibrated only from the blue outer peak and the descriptive peak diagnostics. Keep the SNL-0 same-arc geometry reference and do not promote its candidate mirror arms to a validated detailed reference.
