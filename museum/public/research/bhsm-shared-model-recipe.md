## Common-border normalization

and orthogonal response h. With descriptor s, set t=s/b and k=psi+t h.
The already established identities psi·psi=1 and psi·h=0 give

```
Q = 1 + sum_i (w_i^2-1) k_i^2 + t^2 (h·h+c·c) >= 1,
N = (t c, w k, C+t J),
nu = sqrt(Q),
rate = N/nu.
```

All metric weights used in this identity are at least one. The positive
common b is canceled before differentiation. No independently supported

```

nu_v  = Q_v/(2 nu),
nu_uv = (Q_uv/2 - nu_u nu_v)/nu,

r_v   = (N_v - nu_v r)/nu,
r_uv  = (N_uv - nu_uv r - nu_u r_v - nu_v r_u)/nu.
```

## Limits of the affine support backend

Keeping an exact expression graph does not make every enclosure evaluation
correlation complete. The numerical compiler still has three recorded
relaxations:

1. The inherited primal values and DF coefficients lack explicit common
   theta models. Reuse preserves their pointwise authority, but cannot
   reconstruct unavailable coefficient dependence.
2. The preconditioner correction is enclosed by a q-based tail. Its
   component and repeated-argument correlations are not fully modeled.
3. Affine compilation puts higher-order products, including bilinear u/v
   products, into outward tails. The exact products survive in the graph,
   but a support computation importing those tails cannot claim all their
   signed cancellations have been used.

The new numerical loss is identifiable in `SparseAffine.__mul__`: the
product of two linear directional parts contributes `la*lb` to the scalar
tail. In particular, a nonzero pure u/v bilinear expression can have zero
constant and affine coefficients. Importing that tail into the projected
compiler cannot recover its cancellation with the booked LL/LT polynomial.
The exact multiplication nodes are still serialized; therefore this is a
remaining compiler/representation loss, not proof that an additional
physical action term is intrinsically large. Likewise, the implicit
correction currently uses a supported proposal norm before final output
composition. Its provenance records that limitation explicitly.

The composed output recipe and the numerical upper enclosure are recorded
separately. An enormous surviving upper bound is therefore a representation
diagnostic, not a lower bound on an action-derived physical term. Case 3
of the requested physical-failure classification requires additional
rigorous evidence after all known common dependencies have been retained;
this backend alone cannot establish it.
