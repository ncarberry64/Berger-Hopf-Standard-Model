The museum consequently does not use it to generate a numerical mass readout.
2. [Mass gap](https://github.com/ncarberry64/Berger-Hopf-Standard-Model/blob/main/theory/legacy_sources/v1_1/mass_gap.pdf), especially §§II–III. This is a proposed scalar-EFT analogue, not a proof of the Yang–Mills mass gap. Its displayed quadratic operator is not obtained by substituting φ = φ₀ + η into the preceding Lagrangian under the stated background condition: that substitution yields gradient and biharmonic terms without the displayed k_loc-dependent quadratic terms. The exhibit does not promote its claimed gap to an established physical mass.

These observations explain the choice of the later, explicit relative-energy contract as the equation shown under the author's visual interpretation. They create no new completion obligations.
