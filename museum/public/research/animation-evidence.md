# What drives the museum animations

Reviewed 30 September 2026. Animation speed is a presentation choice unless
an explicit physical readout says otherwise. Motion can reveal a mathematical
construction, traverse measured records, or explain an established mechanism;
it is not automatically a solved physical history.

| Display | Evidence and animated quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome / Hopf geometry | Parameterized S³ circle fibers projected to three dimensions; [implementation](https://github.com/ncarberry64/Berger-Hopf-Standard-Model/blob/main/museum/lib/berger-hopf.ts). View rotation changes projection, not physical state. |
| Children: hypersphere, harmonics, moiré | S³ parameterization, standing-wave sines with fixed nodes, superposed rotated hexagons. [Equations](https://github.com/ncarberry64/Berger-Hopf-Standard-Model/blob/main/museum/lib/children-geometry.ts). These are mathematical constructions; BHSM’s identification with reality remains a proposition. |
| Standard Model block | Selectable reference electric charge and quantum spin. Decorative orbital/rotation markers removed. [CERN overview](https://home.cern/science/physics/standard-model). |
| Hadrons and nuclei | Quark content, schematic gluon exchange, fixed nucleon counts. No calculated QCD trajectories or rotating nucleus. [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/science/doe-explainsquarks-and-gluons). |
| Atoms | Hydrogenic probability-density sections computed from radial/angular functions; viewing angle rotates, density does not orbit. Carbon uses explicitly identified one-electron shape illustrations, not an evaluated many-electron density. [Implementation](https://github.com/ncarberry64/Berger-Hopf-Standard-Model/blob/main/museum/lib/orbital-density.ts). |
| Molecules | H₂O bends about 104.5°, CO₂ is linear with stretching motion; DNA illustrates the double helix. Shapes explain known structure; vibration amplitudes and playback speeds are not spectroscopic measurements. [Bond geometry](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-atoms-first-2e/pages/4-6-molecular-structure-and-polarity). |
| States of matter | Explanatory diagrams of localized solid motion, liquid rearrangement, gas motion and mobile ions/electrons. The automatic selector is a tour of states, not a thermodynamic simulation. [Reference](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/1-2-phases-and-classification-of-matter). |
| Large structures | Schematic cosmic web, a BAO pair-separation excess, and literature-based flow basins. No survey coordinates or calibrated velocities are implied. Sources are linked beside BAO, Laniakea, Great Attractor and Shapley. Decorative star twinkling removed. |
| Berger metric | g = gₕ + s²gᵥ; scalar proxy λ uses the repository equation with a=1/s. No particle mass is inferred from an arbitrarily highlighted fiber. [Metric](https://github.com/ncarberry64/Berger-Hopf-Standard-Model/blob/main/theory/berger_base_action_coupling_normalization.md). |
| Forces | QCD exchange schematic; normalized Coulomb and Newtonian inverse-square curves; known neutron beta-decay products; S³ projection. Invented surface collisions, release rates and moving gravity sheets removed. The BHSM force-line ontology remains static with a guided reading highlight. |
| BHSM matter and mass | Three-family tour uses frozen charged-lepton (k,j) labels and their Hopf phase characters q=k−2j; viewing rotation exposes the phase pattern, including the uniform third-family mode. The mass scene illustrates the author's displaced-energy interpretation with uud quarks, color threads, an enlarged electron cloud and a cleared vacuum-response region. No QCD trajectories, literal virtual-particle fluid or numerical mass calibration are implied. The equation tour follows the matched relative-energy contract. [Sources and exact scope](mass-and-hypersphere-context.md). |
| Magnetic moments | CODATA 2022 magnetic moments drive conventional Larmor frequencies f=2|μ|B/h in chosen B=0.1 T. Arrows illustrate quantum expectation vectors, not spinning material surfaces; time is logarithmically compressed. BHSM local tree result and open quantum correction are explained separately. |
| Collision theatre | Conserved four-momentum calculations with reference masses, or recorded CMS four-vectors. Tracks encode kinematics, not detector hits; no physical cross sections/branching probabilities are invented. [Implementation](https://github.com/ncarberry64/Berger-Hopf-Standard-Model/blob/main/museum/lib/science-media.ts). |
| Original cosmology | Selectable historical storyboard with a three-second guided tour while visible. The cadence presents successive illustrated panels, not computed cosmic dynamics or physical time. Reduced-motion and pause controls stop automatic advancement. The original still illustration remains in an expandable source panel. |
| R1 cosmology | Frozen audited transfer matrices and replay epochs drive the response. User-selected spatial modes and demonstration inputs remain labeled. [Replay](r1-coupled-environment-replay.json). |
| Comparisons | Historical source-bound numbers and reference uncertainties; animated selection is navigation, not model evolution. [Data](../data/sandbox-comparison.json). |
| CMS and numerical research | Measured CMS four-vectors are transformed between charts. The timing replay uses recorded benchmark medians, slowed uniformly 3×; the numerical monitor traverses retained scalar certificates. [Benchmark](https://github.com/ncarberry64/Berger-Hopf-Standard-Model/blob/main/artifacts/cern_open_data_benchmark/results.json). |

No animation promotes an open physical claim. The original sources, existing
completion definition and gate ledger govern the claims.
