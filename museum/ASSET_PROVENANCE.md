# Museum asset provenance

- The five supplementary sandbox panels use the ten complete numerical pairs
  and four qualitative sentinels in
  `../data/museum/bhsm_sandbox_comparison_20260902.json`, copied identically to
  `public/data/sandbox-comparison.json`. The source snapshot is
  `../docs/museum/sandbox_comparison_source_2026-09-02.md`; its normalized
  SHA-256 and original intake hash are embedded in the data. The displayed
  percent differences are a downstream presentation calculation only. Every
  panel labels historical BHSM screens and unverified sandbox references;
  no uncertainty, missing numerical pair or physical promotion is invented.
- Computation and research achievements occupy one separate exhibit.
  Its CMS and residual-check displays, the restored science prototypes and
  the final cosmology exhibits use
  assets synchronized from `../docs/assets/`. The PR #98 animation uses
  CMS dimuon Open Data Record 303; its compact sample, pinned source checksum,
  DOI, CC0 license, and benchmark boundary live under
  `../docs/assets/pr98_cms_open_data_animation/`.
- The CMS animation still is also used as the social preview. Its checked-in
  64-event, 128-vector sample is copied to `public/data/` for the interactive
  selector. Historical exhibit records are indexed by
  `../docs/assets/bhsm_readme_visual_status.json`.
- The former temporary particle spectrum and historical AE2 carrier matrix
  are no longer public exhibit choices. Their source records remain in the
  academic repository and Git history.
- The action landscape, structural spectral scan, form-factor projection,
  collision event display, and decay-channel ledger
  are deterministic normalized explanatory simulations generated by
  `../docs/assets/generate_bhsm_museum_engines.py`. Their plotted values are
  neither experimental measurements nor physical predictions.
- The existing no-fit residual monitor now reads actual BHSM numerical data
  from `../data/museum/bhsm_gate7_scalar_response.json`: 370 local projected
  Hilbert–Schmidt second-residual norm upper bounds from the frozen central
  scalar certificate at source revision
  `d75e77bbdcfba79a83b9c9f9c8c398ae8e7a79d4`. These are numerical bounds, not
  prediction-minus-measurement differences. The generator checks the export's
  LF-normalized SHA-256
  `39EFAA5910D67390A51EAE0FEF077134A8EF281FEAA8927FC5EB6265ADEB56EA`
  and stops on missing or altered data. Existing exhibit files and controls
  are reused; the separate scalar chart is removed. See
  `../docs/bhsm_museum_engine_data_contract.md` for each engine's input gates.
- The cosmology other-work exhibit is a deterministic schematic generated by
  `../docs/assets/generate_cosmology_other_work_exhibit.py` from the model,
  equations, reported parameter ranges, limitations, and falsifiers stated in
  Norman P. Carberry's independently authored preprint,
  DOI `10.20944/preprints202601.1427.v1` (posted 20 January 2026, CC BY 4.0,
  not peer reviewed). The animation is not a survey-data visualization and does
  not establish the proposed cosmological model.
- `public/bhsm-symbol.svg` is reused from the sibling Bubo Research Node project
  (`frontend/src/assets/bhsm-symbol.svg`). That project identifies the mark as
  an original repository asset and publishes it under the MIT License.
- Lucide interface icons are supplied by the declared `lucide-react` package.

- The permanent science collection is generated from existing prediction,
  SM-bundle and current-system artifacts by `../tools/materialize_museum_science.py`.
  Source hashes and classifications remain in `public/data/science-collection.json`.
  The mirrored app JSON supports local development without importing public assets.
- `lib/collision-demo.ts` computes deterministic relativistic two-body kinematics
  for explicitly labeled user-controlled demonstration masses and selected states.
  Its four-vectors are simulated event outputs, not BHSM amplitudes or probabilities.
- The force-unification animation is a conceptual connection diagram, not a
  running-coupling plot. Its historical numeric table retains SCREEN labels.
- `app/cosmic-enclosure.tsx` illustrates Norman's September 7 author brief,
  with Bubble/Wave and evaporation context from his privately supplied
  *The Prints.pptx* (slides 1–2, 7 and 11), as a
  separate speculative other-work exhibit. Surface flows, body markers, explosive
  events, black-hole concentrations, smooth phase and whole-surface release are
  explanatory graphics, not observed or derived cosmic evolution. Context and
  limits: `../docs/museum/norman_cosmic_enclosure_cycle.md`.
